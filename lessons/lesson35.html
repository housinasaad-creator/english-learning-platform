Future Perfect & Continuous

المستقبل التام والمستمر

Learn two advanced future forms: Future Perfect (will have done) to talk about actions completed before a future point, and Future Continuous (will be doing) for actions in progress at a future moment — plus the bonus Future Perfect Continuous for durations.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
📌 Two Advanced Future Forms — شكلان متقدمان للمستقبل
كلاهما يتحدث عن المستقبل — لكن كل واحد يُركّز على جانب مختلف
⏳ Completed before a future time
Future Perfect
المستقبل التام
will have + past participle
An action will be fully completed before a specific future point in time
"By Friday, I will have finished the report." 🔉
"By the time you arrive, she will have left."
▶ In progress at a future moment
Future Continuous
المستقبل المستمر
will be + verb-ing
An action will be in progress at a specific moment in the future
"At 8pm tomorrow, I will be studying." 🔉
"Don't call at noon — I'll be having lunch."
🟣 Future Perfect — التكوين الكامل
will have + التصريف الثالث — لجميع الضمائر بدون استثناء
Future Perfect — Formation
✅ Positive
Subject + will have + past participle
By next month, she will have saved enough money. 🔉
❌ Negative
Subject + won't have + past participle
I won't have finished the project by Monday. 🔉
? Question
Will + subject + have + past participle?
Will you have eaten before we get there? 🔉
Key uses of Future Perfect:
• With by: "By 5pm, I will have sent all the emails."
• With by the time: "By the time he arrives, we will have eaten."
• With before: "She will have graduated before summer."
• With in + time: "In two hours, the film will have ended."
🔵 Future Continuous — التكوين الكامل
will be + الفعل + ing — لجميع الضمائر بدون استثناء
Future Continuous — Formation
✅ Positive
Subject + will be + verb-ing
This time next week, we will be flying to Dubai. 🔉
❌ Negative
Subject + won't be + verb-ing
I won't be working on Saturday. 🔉
? Question
Will + subject + be + verb-ing?
Will you be attending the meeting tomorrow? 🔉
Three uses of Future Continuous:
• In progress at a future time: "At midnight I will be sleeping."
• Expected / planned routine: "She will be leaving at 7 as usual."
• Polite inquiry about plans: "Will you be joining us for dinner?" (softer than "Will you join?")
🔄 Future Perfect vs Future Continuous — الفرق بينهما
نفس نقطة المستقبل — لكن الفعل التام ينتهي قبلها والمستمر يكون جارياً فيها
Focus 🟣 Future Perfect 🔵 Future Continuous
At midnight By midnight, he will have driven 500km. (completed) 🔉 At midnight, he will be driving through the desert. (in progress) 🔉
Tomorrow 3pm By 3pm tomorrow, I will have submitted the report. 🔉 At 3pm tomorrow, I will be presenting the report. 🔉
Next year By next year, they will have built the new school. 🔉 Next year, they will still be building the new school. 🔉
Key signal word by / before / by the time / in at (time) / this time / still / while
Core idea Action is finished before the future point Action is happening at the future point
🟠 Bonus: Future Perfect Continuous — المستقبل التام المستمر
للتعبير عن مدة فعل مستمر سيكون قد استغرق وقتاً معيناً بحلول لحظة مستقبلية
⏱ Future Perfect Continuous
will have been + verb-ing
• Emphasises the duration of an ongoing action up to a future point
• Often used with for + time period and by
"By next June, I will have been studying English for five years." 🔉
"When they retire, they will have been working together for thirty years."
"By the time the meeting ends, we will have been talking for three hours."
⚠ State verbs (know / like / want / believe / understand) do NOT use continuous forms:
❌ "By then, I will have been knowing him for years."  →  ✅ "I will have known him for years."
📅 Time Expressions — الظروف الزمنية
هذه الكلمات تُساعدك على اختيار الزمن الصحيح
🟣 Future Perfect
by · by the time
بحلول / بحلول الوقت الذي
"By Monday, I will have called them." 🔉
🟣 Future Perfect
before · in (time)
قبل / في غضون
"In two hours, it will have ended." 🔉
🔵 Future Continuous
at (time) · at this time
في (وقت محدد)
"At 9am, I'll be driving to work." 🔉
🔵 Future Continuous
this time tomorrow · still
في هذا الوقت غداً / لا يزال
"This time tomorrow I'll be relaxing." 🔉
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
By tomorrow, she finishes the project. → By tomorrow, she will have finished the project.
مع by → لازم future perfect: will have + تصريف ثالث
❌
At 3pm tomorrow, I will study. → At 3pm tomorrow, I will be studying.
فعل جارٍ في لحظة مستقبلية → future continuous: will be + ing
❌
I will have been knowing him for years. → I will have known him for years.
know فعل حالة — لا يأتي بصيغة المستمر أبداً
❌
By the time he arrives, I leave. → By the time he arrives, I will have left.
by the time + present تعني: الفعل الآخر سيكون قد انتهى → future perfect
❌
Will you be joining us? (sounds rude) → Will you be joining us? ✅ (this is actually MORE polite)
future continuous في الأسئلة = أكثر لطفاً من "Will you join us?"
💡 Quick Tips
by → FP
by + a time = almost always Future Perfect: "By 6pm, I will have left." 🔉by + وقت = إشارة قوية جداً للمستقبل التام
this time tomorrow
"This time tomorrow" → Future Continuous: "This time tomorrow I'll be flying to Madrid." 🔉هذا التعبير يُشير دائماً للمستقبل المستمر
Polite ?
Use Future Continuous for polite enquiries: "Will you be using the car?" (softer than "Will you use it?") 🔉المستقبل المستمر في الأسئلة = أكثر لطفاً ودبلوماسية
Duration
For duration up to a future point, use Future Perfect Continuous + for: "I'll have been working here for 5 years." 🔉المدة حتى نقطة مستقبلية → المستقبل التام المستمر + for

موعد تسليم المشروع
Future Perfect — by / won't have

هل ستكون قد أنهيت التصميم بحلول الخميس؟
Manager

لن أكون قد أنهيت كل شيء بحلول الخميس، لكنني سأكون قد أنهيت الأقسام الرئيسية.

لا بأس. بحلول وقت وصول العميل يوم الجمعة، سنكون قد راجعنا كل شيء على أي حال.

ممتاز. سأكون قد أرسلت لك الملفات قبل نهاية اليوم غداً.

📲 Planning a Call
تنسيق موعد مكالمة
Future Continuous — polite inquiry
Will you be free tomorrow afternoon for a quick call? 🔉
هل ستكون متفرغاً بعد ظهر الغد لمكالمة سريعة؟
Lina
I will be attending a training from 2 to 4. But I will be free after that. 🔉
سأكون حاضراً تدريباً من الثانية حتى الرابعة. لكنني سأكون متفرغاً بعد ذلك.
Khalid
Great. Will you be using your laptop or your phone for the call? 🔉
رائع. هل ستستخدم لابتوبك أم هاتفك للمكالمة؟
Lina
My laptop. I will be working from home so the connection will be better. 🔉
اللابتوب. سأكون أعمل من المنزل فسيكون الاتصال أفضل.
Khalid
✈ Holiday Plans
خطط الإجازة
both forms — this time tomorrow
This time tomorrow, we will be sitting on the beach in Bali! 🔉
في هذا الوقت غداً، سنكون جالسين على الشاطئ في بالي!
Noor
I know! By the time we land, we will have been travelling for fourteen hours. 🔉
أعرف! بحلول الوقت الذي نهبط فيه، سنكون قد سافرنا لأربع عشرة ساعة.
Faris
But by the time we check in, they will have prepared the room. So we can relax straight away. 🔉
لكن بحلول وقت تسجيل الوصول، سيكونون قد جهّزوا الغرفة. فيمكننا الاسترخاء مباشرةً.
Noor
Exactly. And tomorrow night, we will be watching the sunset over the ocean! 🔉
بالضبط. وغداً مساءً، سنكون نشاهد الغروب فوق المحيط!
Faris
🎓 Graduation Talk
حديث عن التخرج
Future Perfect — milestones
So by this time next year, you will have graduated and started working? 🔉
إذن بهذا الوقت العام القادم، ستكون قد تخرجت وبدأت العمل؟
Uncle
Hopefully yes! By June I will have finished all my exams. Then I start applying for jobs. 🔉
نأمل ذلك! بحلول يونيو سأكون قد أنهيت كل امتحاناتي. ثم أبدأ التقدم للوظائف.
Rana
By the time you graduate, your father and I will have been waiting for this day for twenty-two years! 🔉
بحلول وقت تخرجك، سنكون أنا وأبوك قد انتظرنا هذا اليوم اثنين وعشرين سنة!
Mother
I know, mum. I promise I won't have let you down. 🔉
أعرف يا أمي. أعدك أنني لن أخيّبكم.
Rana
🏋 The Marathon
ماراثون
Future Perfect Continuous + both
Are you ready for the marathon next month? 🔉
هل أنت مستعد للماراثون الشهر القادم؟
Coach
I think so. By race day, I will have been training for six months. 🔉
أظن ذلك. بحلول يوم السباق، سأكون قد تدربت لمدة ستة أشهر.
Jamal
Excellent. At the halfway point, you will have been running for about two hours. How will you feel? 🔉
ممتاز. عند النقطة النصفية، ستكون قد جريت لمدة ساعتين تقريباً. كيف ستكون حالك؟
Coach
Tired but strong. And when I cross the finish line, I will have achieved something I never thought possible. 🔉
متعباً لكن قوياً. وعندما أعبر خط النهاية، سأكون قد حققت شيئاً لم أكن أظنه ممكناً.
Jamal
📘 The Launch Day
يوم الإطلاق

By the time the doors open at nine, the team will have been working on this product launch for eighteen months. 🔉

At this very moment tomorrow, hundreds of customers will be queuing outside the new store and journalists will be filming the event live. 🔉

Sara, the project director, says she hopes that by launch day, the engineering team will have solved the last software issue. 🔉

By the time the first customers enter, staff will have been standing at their positions for two hours, fully briefed and ready to assist. 🔉

The CEO will be giving a short speech at midday. By then, they will have announced the product price and revealed the key features. 🔉

By the end of launch day, the company will have sold more units than they sold in the entire first year of the previous model. 🔉
