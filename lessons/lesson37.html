Causative: have / get done

الأفعال السببية

The causative tells us that someone arranged for something to be done — rather than doing it themselves. Master three key structures: have something done, get something done, and have / get someone do something — and know when to use each one.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
📌 What is the Causative?
ما هو الفعل السببي؟
🔧 Someone else does it — for you
The causative is used when the subject arranges for another person to perform an action.
The subject does NOT do the action — they organise, pay for, or cause it to be done.
✏ Did it yourself
"I cut my hair." 🔉
قصّيت شعري بنفسي
✂ Someone else did it
"I had my hair cut." 🔉
قصّ لي شخص آخر شعري (حلاق)
🧩 Three Causative Structures
ثلاثة أنماط للفعل السببي
🟠 Neutral / Formal
have + obj + past participle
have + O + pp
Most common. You arrange a service. Works in all tenses.
"I had my car repaired." 🔉
"She has her nails done every week."
"We had the house painted last month."
🔵 Informal / Effort involved
get + obj + past participle
get + O + pp
Informal version of "have done". Implies more effort to arrange it.
"I got my laptop fixed." 🔉
"You should get your eyes tested."
"We need to get the roof repaired."
🟣 Direct someone
have/get + person + verb
have + person + base / get + person + to-inf
have + person + base verb (direct order)
get + person + to-inf (persuade / arrange)
"I'll have the technician check it." 🔉
"She got the plumber to come early."
"He had his assistant send the files."
📋 Full Formation — have something done
التكوين الكامل لـ have + object + past participle
have + object + past participle — all forms
✅ Positive
Subject + have/has + object + past participle
She has her car serviced every six months. 🔉
❌ Negative
Subject + don't/doesn't/didn't have + object + past participle
I didn't have my suit cleaned before the interview. 🔉
? Question
Do/Does/Did + subject + have + object + past participle?
Did you have your passport renewed? 🔉
🕒 Causative in Different Tenses
الفعل السببي في مختلف الأزمنة
Present Simple
have/has + obj + pp
"She has her nails done every week." 🔉
تفعل ذلك بشكل منتظم
Past Simple
had + obj + pp
"He had his screen replaced yesterday." 🔉
حدث في الماضي
Future (will)
will have + obj + pp
"I'll have the flat cleaned before they arrive." 🔉
سيحدث في المستقبل
Present Continuous
am/is/are having + obj + pp
"We're having the kitchen renovated." 🔉
يجري الآن / تم ترتيبه
Present Perfect
have/has had + obj + pp
"She has had her hair coloured." 🔉
مرتبط بالحاضر
Going to
going to have/get + obj + pp
"We're going to get the pipes checked." 🔉
خطة مستقبلية
🔄 Active vs Causative vs Passive
الفعل المعلوم مقابل السببي مقابل المجهول
Focus 🔵 Active 🟠 Causative 🟣 Passive
Who does it The subject does it themselves Someone else does it — for the subject Unknown / unimportant who does it
Car repair A mechanic repaired my car. 🔉 I had my car repaired. 🔉 My car was repaired. 🔉
Hair cut She cut her own hair. 🔉 She had her hair cut. 🔉 Her hair was cut. 🔉
House painting He painted the house. 🔉 He had the house painted. 🔉 The house was painted. 🔉
Key difference Subject = doer Subject = arranger — not the doer Doer unknown or not important
⚠ Unfortunate Causative — السببي للمصائب
أحياناً يُستخدم "have something done" لأشياء وقعت علينا بدون أن نرتّبها
🔴 When bad things happen TO you
"have something done" can also describe an unfortunate event — something that happened to the subject, not arranged by them. Context makes the meaning clear.
• "She had her bag stolen on the bus." (= someone stole it — not her arrangement!) 🔉
• "He had his car broken into last night."
• "They had their house flooded during the storm."
• "I had my phone hacked."
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
I had my hair cut it. → I had my hair cut.
لا تضيف ضمير بعد التصريف الثالث في الفعل السببي
❌
I had my car to repair. → I had my car repaired.
بعد have + object لازم past participle — ليس to-infinitive
❌
She got her nails do. → She got her nails done.
get + object + past participle — ليس base verb
❌
I got the technician fixed it. → I got the technician to fix it.
get + person + to-infinitive — ليس past participle
❌
I had the technician to check it. → I had the technician check it.
have + person + base verb (بدون to) — مختلف عن get + person + to-inf
💡 Quick Tips
have vs get
have done = neutral/formal  |  get done = informal, implies more effort to arrange 🔉have done أكثر رسمية / get done أكثر عامية وجهداً
person + verb
have + person + base verb: "I'll have him call you."  |  get + person + to-inf: "I'll get him to call you." 🔉have + شخص + فعل أساسي | get + شخص + to + فعل
All tenses ✅
The causative works in every tense — just change have/get: had done / will have done / is having done 🔉الفعل السببي يعمل في كل الأزمنة — فقط غيّر have أو get
Bad events
"have sth done" can also describe bad events that happened to you (not arranged): "I had my wallet stolen." 🔉يُستخدم أيضاً للمصائب التي وقعت عليك بدون ترتيب

عند الحلاق
have something done

شعرك يبدو رائعاً! هل قصصته بنفسك؟
Leila
No, I had it cut at that new salon downtown. And I had it coloured too — do you like it? 🔉
لا، قصّيته في ذلك الصالون الجديد في وسط المدينة. وصبّغته أيضاً — هل يعجبك؟
Rania
I love it! How often do you have your hair done? 🔉
أحبه! كم مرة تقومين بعمل شعرك؟
Leila
About once a month. I'm also going to have my nails done there next week. 🔉
مرة في الشهر تقريباً. وسأعمل أظافري هناك أيضاً الأسبوع القادم.

مشكلة في السيارة
get something done / have done
My car has been making a strange noise for a week. I think the engine needs checking. 🔉
سيارتي تصدر صوتاً غريباً منذ أسبوع. أظن المحرك يحتاج فحصاً.
Faisal
You need to get it checked as soon as possible. Don't leave it. 🔉
تحتاج إلى فحصها في أقرب وقت. لا تتركها.
Sami
I know. I also need to get the oil changed and the tyres rotated. 🔉
أعرف. أحتاج أيضاً تغيير الزيت وتدوير الإطارات.
Faisal
My mechanic is very good. I've had my car serviced by him for five years. I'll give you his number. 🔉
ميكانيكي جيد جداً. أصلّح سيارتي عنده منذ خمس سنوات. سأعطيك رقمه.

🏠 Home Renovation
تجديد المنزل
have done — multiple tenses
Your apartment looks so different! What happened? 🔉
شقتك تبدو مختلفة جداً! ماذا حدث؟
Hana
We had the walls repainted, had new floors fitted, and had the bathroom completely renovated. 🔉
أعدنا طلاء الجدران، ووضعنا أرضيات جديدة، وجدّدنا الحمام بالكامل.
Tariq
Wow. Are you still having work done or is it finished? 🔉
واو. هل لا تزالون تجدّدون أم انتهيتم؟
Hana
Almost done. We're still going to have the kitchen cupboards replaced next week. 🔉
شارفنا على الانتهاء. سنستبدل خزائن المطبخ الأسبوع القادم.

📋 At the Office
في المكتب
have/get someone do something
The report needs to be translated into Arabic by tomorrow. Can you handle it? 🔉
يجب ترجمة التقرير إلى العربية بحلول الغد. هل تستطيع التعامل معه؟
Director
Leave it with me. I'll have our translator do it this afternoon. 🔉
اتركها معي. سأطلب من مترجمنا أن يقوم بها هذا المساء.
Sara
Also, can you get someone to print fifty copies of the presentation? 🔉
أيضاً، هل يمكنك أن تطلبي من أحد طباعة خمسين نسخة من العرض؟
Director
Of course. I'll get Khalid to do that right away. 🔉
بالطبع. سأطلب من خالد أن يفعل ذلك فوراً.
Sara

📘 Moving In
الانتقال إلى منزل جديد

Reem had just bought her first apartment and there was a lot to organise before moving in. 🔉

First, she had the whole flat professionally cleaned. Then she had the locks changed for security — she didn't know how many keys the previous owners had. 🔉

She had always wanted a modern kitchen, so she got the old cupboards replaced and had new appliances installed by an electrician. 🔉

Her brother helped her get the furniture delivered. She got him to assemble the wardrobe while she had the curtains fitted by a specialist. 🔉

A week before moving in, Reem had her internet connection set up and had a new air conditioning unit installed. 🔉

Finally, she had a professional photographer take photos of the finished apartment. Everything had been arranged — and she had not done any of it with her own hands. 🔉
