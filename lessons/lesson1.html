My First Day in English Class

Sam is a new student. It is his first day in English class. The teacher's name is Mrs. White. She is very kind.

"Today," says Mrs. White, "we learn the English alphabet. There are twenty-six letters in English — from A to Z."

Sam opens his notebook. He writes carefully: A, B, C, D, E, F, G ... all the way to Z. It takes three minutes.

"Now," asks Mrs. White, "how many letters are vowels?"

Sam thinks for a moment. "A, E, I, O, U — five vowels!" he says.

"Excellent!" says Mrs. White. "And the other twenty-one are consonants."

After the alphabet, they learn numbers. Mrs. White writes on the board: 1, 2, 3 ... up to 100. She points to the number 35.

"Can you say this number?" she asks. Sam looks. "Thirty-five!" he answers.

"Perfect!" says Mrs. White. "Remember — always use a hyphen: thirty-five, not 'thirty five'."

At the end of class, Mrs. White smiles. "Next week, you will learn to spell your names. Practice at home tonight!"

Sam is very happy. He can say the alphabet and count to one hundred. English is fun!
