Cleft Sentences

الجمل المشقوقة

A cleft sentence splits a single idea into two clauses to place extra emphasis on one element. Instead of saying "John broke the window," you say "It was John who broke the window." Master four types: It-cleft, What-cleft, All-cleft, and Reversed cleft — essential tools for academic writing and persuasive speech.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
✂ What is a Cleft Sentence?
ما هي الجملة المشقوقة؟
A cleft sentence "cleaves" (splits) one sentence into two clauses using a dummy subject or wh-word — this moves one element into a prominent, emphatic position.

Normal:   "Ali solved the problem yesterday."
Cleft:   "It was yesterday that Ali solved the problem." ← emphasises WHEN
Cleft:   "It was Ali who solved the problem." ← emphasises WHO
Cleft:   "What Ali solved was the problem." ← emphasises WHAT
🟡 Type 1
It-cleft
It + be + FOCUS + that/who + rest
"It was Ali who solved the problem."
🔵 Type 2
What-cleft (Pseudo-cleft)
What + S + V + be + FOCUS
"What I need is more time."
🟢 Type 3
All-cleft
All + S + V + be + FOCUS
"All I want is a fair chance." (restrictive)
🟣 Type 4
Reversed Cleft
FOCUS + be + what + S + V
"Dedication is what separates the great."
🟡 Type 1 — It-cleft
النوع الأول: تقديم بـ It is / It was
Structure: It + is/was + FOCUS element + that / who / which + rest of sentence

• Use who when the focus is a person
• Use that when the focus is a thing, time, place, or reason
• Match the tense of be to the time of the sentence
Normal
Sarah designed the new logo.
→
Emphasise WHO
🔉 It was Sarah who designed the new logo.
Normal
They found the error in the final report.
→
Emphasise WHERE
🔉 It was in the final report that they found the error.
Normal
The power cut caused the system failure.
→
Emphasise CAUSE
🔉 It was the power cut that caused the system failure.
Normal
The board approved the budget last Tuesday.
→
Emphasise WHEN
🔉 It was last Tuesday that the board approved the budget.
🎯 Any element can be the FOCUS — who, what, when, where, why, how:
Focus: WHO
"It was the CEO who announced the merger." 🔉
Focus: WHAT
"It was the budget that concerned them most." 🔉
Focus: WHEN
"It was in 1969 that humans first walked on the moon." 🔉
Focus: WHERE
"It was in this room that the treaty was signed." 🔉
Focus: WHY
"It was because of the delay that clients lost trust." 🔉
Negative
"It is not the price that worries me — it's the quality." 🔉
🔵 Type 2 — What-cleft (Pseudo-cleft)
النوع الثاني: تقديم بـ What
Structure: What + subject + verb + is/was + FOCUS

• The "what" clause acts as the subject of the sentence
• The FOCUS (what you want to emphasise) comes after is/was
• "What he did was (to) apologise." — the FOCUS can be an infinitive after an action verb
Emphasise a THING 🔉
"What this company needs is a clear strategy and better communication."
Emphasise an ACTION 🔉
"What he did was apologise immediately and offer a full refund."
Emphasise a FEELING 🔉
"What surprised everyone was how calmly she handled the crisis."
Emphasise UNDERSTANDING 🔉
"What I don't understand is why nobody flagged the problem earlier."
Negative — Emphasise CONTRAST 🔉
"What we lack is not funding — what we lack is direction."
🟢🟣 Types 3 & 4 — All-cleft & Reversed Cleft
النوع الثالث والرابع
• All-cleft: same as what-cleft but more restrictive — "this and nothing more"
• Reversed cleft: FOCUS moves to the front — very emphatic, used in speeches
What-cleft
"What I want is recognition."
→
All-cleft (more restrictive)
🔉 "All I want is recognition — nothing more."
Normal
"You only need to sign here."
→
All-cleft
🔉 "All you need to do is sign here."
What-cleft
"What separates the great is dedication."
→
Reversed cleft (FOCUS first)
🔉 "Dedication is what separates the great from the average."
Normal
"Transparency is what we stand for."
→
Reversed cleft
🔉 "Transparency — that is what this organisation stands for."
📊 It-cleft vs What-cleft — Key Differences
الفرق بين النوعين الرئيسيين
FeatureIt-cleftWhat-cleft
Structure It + is/was + FOCUS + that/who + rest What + S + V + is/was + FOCUS
FOCUS position Middle of sentence End of sentence
Best for Identifying who/what/when/where/why Emphasising actions or states
Example "It was the manager who approved it." 🔉 "What the manager did was approve it." 🔉
Negative "It was not the price that worried us." 🔉 "What did not worry us was the price." 🔉
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
It was John that he fixed the car. → It was John who fixed the car.
عند التركيز على شخص: استخدم "who" وليس "that he" — لا يوجد ضمير إضافي
❌
It is yesterday that I saw her. → It was yesterday that I saw her.
زمن "be" يجب أن يتطابق مع زمن الجملة — الماضي يستلزم "was"
❌
What I need them is more resources. → What I need is more resources.
في الـ What-cleft: لا تضع ضميراً زائداً بعد الفعل
❌
All what I want is peace. → All I want is peace.
بعد "All" لا تضع "what" — All يأتي مباشرةً قبل الضمير أو الاسم
❌
It was him who solved the problem. → It was he who solved the problem. (formal) / It was him that solved it. (informal)
في الكتابة الرسمية: It was he/she/they (مبتدأ) + who — في الكلام العادي يُقبل "him/her"
💡 Quick Tips
It-cleft
Use It-cleft to point a finger — to clarify who/what/when/where exactly 🔉استخدم It-cleft للتوضيح الدقيق — مَن بالضبط، ماذا، متى، وأين
What-cleft
Use What-cleft to emphasise an action or state: what someone did, needs, or feels 🔉استخدم What-cleft للتأكيد على فعل أو حالة — ما فعله شخص ما أو ما يحتاجه
All-cleft
All-cleft is more restrictive: "All I need is rest" = only rest, nothing else 🔉All-cleft أكثر تقييداً من What — تعني "هذا فقط ولا شيء غيره"
who vs that
In It-clefts: who for people, that for things/times/places/reasons 🔉who للأشخاص / that للأشياء والأزمنة والأماكن والأسباب

🤔 What We Really Need
ما نحتاجه فعلاً
What I need / What surprised / What we lack
The team keeps missing targets. What do you think is going wrong? 🔉
الفريق يفوّت الأهداف باستمرار. ما الذي تعتقد أنه يسير بشكل خاطئ؟
Director
What we lack is not effort — what we lack is a clear, shared plan. Everyone is working hard but in different directions. 🔉
ما ينقصنا ليس الجهد — بل الخطة الواضحة المشتركة. الجميع يعمل بجد لكن في اتجاهات مختلفة.
Team Lead
What surprised me in last month's report was how much time was being spent on non-priority tasks. 🔉
ما فاجأني في تقرير الشهر الماضي كان حجم الوقت المُنفَق على مهام غير ذات أولوية.
Director
What I need from you is a two-page priority framework by Friday. That is all. 🔉
ما أحتاجه منك هو إطار أولويات من صفحتين بحلول الجمعة. هذا فقط.
Director

أخبرني أحدهم أنك انتقدت عرضي أمام الفريق بأكمله.

ما قصدته كان المساعدة — لا إحراجك. أعتذر على التوقيت.

سيداتي وسادتي، هذا الفريق هو من حوّل موجزاً مستحيلاً إلى حملة فازت بجائزة.

ما نشعر به الآن هو فخر هائل. اللحظات كهذه هي ما يذكّرنا بسبب ما نفعله.

📘 The Turning Point
نقطة التحول

Historians often debate what truly drives social change. It is rarely a single dramatic event that reshapes a society — rather, it is the accumulation of small, overlooked moments that eventually tips the balance. 🔉

Consider the labour movement of the early twentieth century. It was not the strikes themselves that changed legislation — it was the public's growing awareness of unsafe conditions that forced politicians to act. What ordinary workers demanded was dignity, not revolution. 🔉

It was in 1911, following the Triangle Shirtwaist Factory fire, that American labour law underwent its most significant reform. One hundred and forty-six lives were lost — and it was that tragedy that finally made safety legislation unavoidable. 🔉

What the historians remind us is that change rarely arrives on schedule. All we can do is recognise the turning points when they occur — and act with the clarity that the moment demands. 🔉

Courage — that is what history ultimately asks of us. Not perfection, not certainty — simply the willingness to act when it matters most. 🔉
