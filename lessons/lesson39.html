Speculation: must / might / can't

التخمين والاستنتاج

Use modal verbs to express how certain you are about something — in the present and in the past. Master must (almost certain), might / may / could (possible), and can't (impossible), then add must have / might have / can't have for past deductions.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
🤔 What is Speculation?
ما هو التخمين والاستنتاج؟
When we don't know something for certain, we use modal verbs to speculate — to express how sure or unsure we are based on logic or evidence.

• "The lights are off. She must be asleep." (very sure)
• "He's late. He might be stuck in traffic." (possible)
• "He passed without studying. That can't be true!" (impossible)
📊 Certainty Scale
مقياس اليقين
How sure are you?
❌ can't / couldn't
🤔 might / may / could
✅ must
← impossible (0%) possible (~50%) almost certain (90%) →
🧠 Three Core Speculation Modals — Present
ثلاثة أفعال أساسية للتخمين — المضارع
🟢 Almost certain (+)
must
~90% sure
You are very sure something is true based on evidence or logic.
must + base verb
"She must be tired — she worked all day." 🔉
"That must be his car — he always parks here."
"You've eaten nothing — you must be starving."
🔵 Possible / Uncertain
might / may / could
~50% sure
You think it is possible but you are not sure. All three are similar in meaning.
might/may/could + base verb
"He might be in a meeting." 🔉
"She may be running late."
"They could be stuck in traffic."
🔴 Almost certain (−)
can't / couldn't
~0% (impossible)
You are very sure something is NOT true — it seems logically impossible.
can't/couldn't + base verb
"He can't be at home — I saw him downtown." 🔉
"That can't be right — the numbers don't match."
"She couldn't be asleep — her lights are on."
🕓 Present vs Past Speculation
التخمين في المضارع والماضي
For past deductions, add have + past participle after the modal.
• Present: "She must be tired."  →  Past: "She must have been tired."
• Present: "He might be home."  →  Past: "He might have been home."
Modal Present (now / general) Past (already happened)
must She must be exhausted. 🔉 She must have been exhausted. 🔉
might He might be in the office. 🔉 He might have left early. 🔉
may She may be on her way. 🔉 She may have taken a different route. 🔉
could They could be waiting outside. 🔉 They could have missed the announcement. 🔉
can't That can't be true. 🔉 He can't have forgotten — I reminded him. 🔉
couldn't She couldn't be the manager — she just started. 🔉 She couldn't have known about the plan. 🔉
⚠ must = Deduction vs Obligation
must للاستنتاج مقابل must للإلزام
The word must has two different uses — always check the context!
🤔 must = Deduction (speculation)
"You must be joking — that price is insane!" 🔉
"She must be the new manager — everyone respects her."
"He must have worked all night — he looks terrible."
Based on evidence → you are drawing a logical conclusion
📋 must = Obligation (rule / duty)
"You must submit the report by Monday." 🔉
"Students must wear uniforms."
"All visitors must sign in at reception."
An external rule or strong personal advice → obligation
❌ can't = Deduction (impossible)
"That can't be right — she never lies." 🔉
"He can't have won — he didn't even enter."
You are sure it is logically impossible
🚫 don't have to = Not necessary
"You don't have to come if you're busy." 🔉
"She doesn't have to wear a uniform."
No obligation — it is optional (NOT the same as can't!)
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
He must to be tired. → He must be tired.
بعد جميع الـ modals مباشرة base verb — بدون to
❌
She might been late. → She might have been late.
للماضي: modal + have + past participle — لا تحذف have
❌
They must be arrived already. → They must have arrived already.
للتخمين في الماضي استخدم must have + past participle
❌
He can't have be there. → He can't have been there.
بعد have تأتي past participle — been وليس be
❌
She could be at home yesterday. → She could have been at home yesterday.
للكلام عن الماضي: could have been — ليس could be
💡 Quick Tips
must vs can't
must = almost certainly TRUE  ↔  can't = almost certainly NOT TRUE — they are logical opposites 🔉must وcan't عكسان منطقيان — يُستخدمان عند اليقين القوي
Past = + have
For past deductions, always use: modal + have + pp: "must have left / might have forgotten" 🔉للتخمين في الماضي: modal + have + past participle دائماً
might ≈ may ≈ could
might, may, could all express possibility — nearly interchangeable for speculation 🔉الثلاثة تعبّر عن الاحتمال — الفروق بينها طفيفة
Context matters
must (deduction) = based on evidence  |  must (obligation) = rule/duty — context tells the difference 🔉must للاستنتاج تبنى على الدليل | must للإلزام تبنى على قاعدة — السياق يُفرّق

رأيت ليلى؟ لا تردّ على هاتفها.

ربما تكون في المكتبة. تذهب إليها كل ثلاثاء بعد الظهر.

لا يمكن أن تكون في المكتبة — إنها مغلقة اليوم للتجديد.

إذاً يجب أن تكون في المقهى المجاور. دائماً تذهب إليه حين تُغلق المكتبة.

أو ربما يكون قد رأى الأسئلة مسبقاً. قد يكون المعلم أعطى تلميحات.

أشك في ذلك. يجب أن يكون موهوباً في المادة. بعض الناس موهوبون بطبيعتهم.

Nadia

يجب أن يكون الجيران يحركون الأثاث مجدداً. كانوا يُعيدون الديكور طوال الأسبوع.

كان من المفترض أن يصل طردي قبل ثلاثة أيام. لم يصل بعد.

ربما يكون السائق قد تركه عند جار. هل تحققت؟
Mona

نعم، لا أحد لديه. قد يكون أُرسل إلى عنوان خاطئ عن طريق الخطأ.

أو يجب أنه ضاع في المستودع. يحدث هذا مع الطلبات الكبيرة في موسم العطل.

must / may / can't + mixed tenses

فارس استقال دون سابق إنذار. لم يتوقعه أحد.

He must have had a very good reason. People don't just resign from a senior position like that. 🔉
لا بد أن لديه سبباً وجيهاً جداً. الناس لا يستقيلون من مناصب رفيعة هكذا.

ربما تلقّى عرضاً أفضل في مكان آخر. أو ربما كان لديه خلاف مع مجلس الإدارة.
Lara

لا يمكن أن يكون قد غادر بسبب المال فقط — كان يكسب أكثر من أي أحد هنا.
Yousuf
📘 The Empty House
البيت الفارغ

When Dina arrived at her aunt's house, she found the front door wide open and the lights still on inside. 🔉

Her aunt must have left in a hurry — her coat and keys were still on the table, and a half-eaten breakfast sat on the kitchen counter. 🔉

Dina called her cousin Rana. "Something must be wrong," she said. "She can't have gone far without her keys." 🔉

Rana thought for a moment. "She might have heard something outside and run out to check. Or she may have gone to a neighbour's place for help." 🔉

Dina looked around carefully. There were no signs of a break-in. The valuables were untouched. "It can't have been a robbery," she thought. 🔉

Then she noticed a note on the fridge that she had missed. It said: "Gone to hospital with Mrs Kareem next door. Back by noon. Don't worry!" 🔉
