Days & Telling the Time

الأيام وإخبار الوقت

Learn the days of the week, months of the year, and how to say and ask about the time in English.

⏰ Telling the Time — إخبار الوقت
كيف نقول الساعة بالإنجليزية — اضغط على كل بطاقة للاستماع
Asking the time:  What time is it?  What's the time?  Do you have the time?
3:00
It's three o'clock
الساعة الثالثة تماماً
3:30
It's half past three
الساعة الثالثة والنصف
3:15
It's quarter past three
الساعة الثالثة والربع
3:45
It's quarter to four
الساعة الرابعة إلا ربع
3:10
It's ten past three
الثالثة وعشر دقائق
3:20
It's twenty past three
الثالثة وعشرون دقيقة
3:40
It's twenty to four
الرابعة إلا عشرين دقيقة
3:55
It's five to four
الرابعة إلا خمس دقائق
📌 Key Rule:
• Minutes 1–30: use past → "ten past two" (2:10)
• Minutes 31–59: use to → "twenty to three" (2:40)
• Exactly on the hour: use o'clock → "five o'clock" (5:00)
من 1 إلى 30 دقيقة نستخدم past، ومن 31 إلى 59 نستخدم to (نحسب الدقائق للساعة القادمة)
am / pm:
• am (ante meridiem) = صباحاً — midnight to noon (12:00 am → 11:59 am)
• pm (post meridiem) = مساءً — noon to midnight (12:00 pm → 11:59 pm)
• "The meeting is at 9 am."  |  "Dinner is at 7 pm."

📌 Preposition Rule:
• at + time → at 5 o'clock, at noon, at midnight
• on + day / date → on Monday, on the 3rd of June
• in + month / year / season → in July, in 2025, in summer
Dialogue 01
What Time Is It? ⏰
Two friends meet in the street
Rania
Excuse me, do you have the time?
عفواً، هل معك الوقت؟
Hassan
Yes! It's quarter past ten.
نعم! الساعة العاشرة والربع.
Rania
Oh no, I'm late! My class starts at half past ten.
آه لا، تأخرت! صفي يبدأ الساعة العاشرة والنصف.
Hassan
You can still make it! The school is five minutes away.
لا زلت تستطيعين! المدرسة على بعد خمس دقائق.
Rania
Thank you! See you tomorrow!
شكراً! أراك غداً!

هل لديك أي خطط؟

صباح الجمعة أذهب للسوق. بعد ظهر السبت أزور عائلتي.

Dialogue 03
My Daily Routine 🌞
A student describes their day
Teacher
Tell me about your daily routine, Karim.

I wake up at six o'clock. Breakfast is at half past six.
أستيقظ الساعة السادسة. الفطور الساعة السادسة والنصف.
Karim
School starts at eight am. Lunch is at one pm. School finishes at quarter past three.
المدرسة تبدأ الثامنة صباحاً. الغداء الواحدة مساءً. تنتهي الثالثة والربع.
Karim
In the evening I do homework. I go to bed at ten o'clock at night.
في المساء أنجز الواجبات. أنام الساعة العاشرة ليلاً.

That's a very busy day! Well done!
يوم مزدحم جداً! أحسنت!

🔊 استمع للنص:

A Busy Week for Nadia

Nadia is a student. She has a very busy week. Every day she wakes up at seven o'clock in the morning. She has breakfast at half past seven and leaves home at eight o'clock.

On Monday, she has English class from nine am to eleven am. On Tuesday she goes to the library at two in the afternoon. She studies there until five o'clock.

On Wednesday, Nadia has a doctor's appointment at quarter past ten. After the appointment, she meets her friend Sara for lunch at noon.

On Thursday evening, she does her homework from seven pm to nine pm. She goes to bed at half past ten at night.

On Friday and Saturday, Nadia rests. She loves weekends. In the morning she reads books. In the afternoon she visits her family. Her grandmother's birthday is on the 15th of March — this Friday!

Nadia says, "Time is important. I always check the time so I am never late!"
