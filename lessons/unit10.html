Object Pronouns — ضمائر المفعول به

me · you · him · her · it · us · them

I love him · She called me · We know them · He gave us the book

👤 Object Pronouns — نظرة عامة

ضمير المفعول به يأتي بعد الفعل أو بعد حرف الجر — يحل محل الاسم كمفعول به

ضمائر المفعول به — Object Pronouns · تأتي بعد الفعل أو حرف الجر

👤 Subject vs Object Pronouns

الفرق بين ضمير الفاعل وضمير المفعول به — الفاعل قبل الفعل، المفعول بعد الفعل

Subject (فاعل) Object (مفعول به) مثال بالفاعل مثال بالمفعول
ImeI love pizza.She loves me.
youyouYou know him.He knows you.
hehimHe called her.She called him.
sheherShe saw us.We saw her.
ititIt is big.I like it.
weusWe live here.They know us.
theythemThey came early.I called them.
القاعدة: الفاعل (I/he/she/we/they) يأتي قبل الفعل · المفعول (me/him/her/us/them) يأتي بعد الفعل أو بعد حرف الجر

🔵 Singular Object Pronouns — المفرد

ضمائر المفعول به للمفرد: me · you · him · her · it

me — إياي / لي (بعد I)
🔊
She called me yesterday.
هي اتصلت بي أمس.
🔊
Can you help me, please?
هل يمكنك مساعدتي؟
🔊
Give it to me.
أعطِه لي.
him — إياه / له (بعد he)
🔊
I know him very well.
أعرفه جيداً.
🔊
She loves him.
هي تحبه.
🔊
Tell him the news.
أخبره بالأخبار.
her — إياها / لها (بعد she)
🔊
I met her at school.
قابلتها في المدرسة.
🔊
We invited her to the party.
دعوناها إلى الحفلة.
🔊
He sent her a message.
أرسل لها رسالة.
it — إياه (للأشياء والحيوانات)
🔊
I like this book. I read it every day.
أحب هذا الكتاب. أقرأه كل يوم.
🔊
We have a cat. We love it.
لدينا قطة. نحبها.

🟢 Plural Object Pronouns — الجمع

ضمائر المفعول به للجمع: us · you · them

us — إيانا / لنا (بعد we)
🔊
He taught us English last year.
علّمنا اللغة الإنجليزية العام الماضي.
🔊
Can you help us find the station?
هل يمكنك مساعدتنا في إيجاد المحطة؟
🔊
She visited us last weekend.
زارتنا نهاية الأسبوع الماضي.
them — إياهم / لهم (بعد they)
🔊
I called them this morning.
اتصلت بهم هذا الصباح.
🔊
Do you know them?
هل تعرفهم؟
🔊
She gave them a gift.
أعطتهم هدية.
🔊
We saw them at the cinema.
رأيناهم في السينما.
you (plural) — إياكم / لكم
🔊
I will call you (all) tomorrow.
سأتصل بكم غداً.
🔊
She knows you both very well.
هي تعرفكما جيداً.

💬 Common Uses — الاستخدامات الشائعة

ضمير المفعول به يأتي في موقعين رئيسيين: بعد الفعل مباشرة وبعد حرف الجر

After a verb (بعد فعل) Subject + Verb + Object Pronoun I love her. · He called us.
After a preposition (بعد حرف جر) ... preposition + Object Pronoun Give it to me. · He talked about them.
Two objects (فعل + مفعولان) Verb + person + thing She gave him a book. · Tell me the answer.
After verbs — بعد الأفعال
🔊
I can see him from here.
أستطيع رؤيته من هنا.
🔊
We invited her to dinner.
دعوناها إلى العشاء.
🔊
They called us twice.
اتصلوا بنا مرتين.
After prepositions — بعد حروف الجر (to/for/with/about/at)
🔊
She is talking to him.
هي تتحدث إليه.
🔊
He bought a gift for her.
اشترى هدية لها.
🔊
Can you come with us?
هل يمكنك القدوم معنا؟
🔊
She is thinking about them.
هي تفكر فيهم.
🔊
He looked at me and smiled.
نظر إليّ وابتسم.

⭐ Tips & Tricks — نصائح وملاحظات

أهم الملاحظات والفروق الدقيقة في ضمائر المفعول به

💡 her = possessive too her (possessive) = كتابها I see her. (object) vs. her book (possessive)
💡 you is always you you (subject) = you (object) You know me. / I know you. — same word!
💡 it replaces things things / animals → it I have a dog. I love it. (not him/her usually)
💡 Listen to / wait for verb + prep + object Listen to me. · I'm waiting for you.
التمييز بين "her" كمفعول وكصفة ملكية
🔊
I know her. / This is her bag.
أعرفها. / هذه حقيبتها. — نفس الكلمة، معنيان مختلفان
Prepositional phrases — عبارات حرف الجر
🔊
Listen to me carefully.
استمع إليّ بعناية.
🔊
I am waiting for you.
أنا أنتظرك.
🔊
She wrote a letter to him.
كتبت له رسالة.
🔊
He is looking at us.
هو ينظر إلينا.
🔊
We talked about them all day.
تحدثنا عنهم طوال اليوم.

⚠️ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة

تجنّب هذه الأخطاء الشائعة في ضمائر المفعول به

He loves she.
He loves her.
بعد الفعل نستخدم ضمير المفعول "her" وليس ضمير الفاعل "she"
Give it to I.
Give it to me.
بعد حرف الجر "to" نستخدم "me" وليس "I" — حرف الجر دائماً يتبعه ضمير المفعول
Me like him.
I like him.
"I" هو ضمير الفاعل يأتي قبل الفعل — "me" يأتي فقط بعد الفعل أو حرف الجر
He called they yesterday.
He called them yesterday.
بعد الفعل "called" نستخدم "them" وليس "they"
We saw he at the mall.
We saw him at the mall.
كلمة "he" لا تأتي بعد الفعل — نستخدم "him" كمفعول به
She is talking to we.
She is talking to us.
بعد حرف الجر "to" نستخدم "us" وليس "we"

Dialogue 01
Talking About a Friend
Two friends talking about someone they know — صديقتان تتحدثان عن شخص يعرفانه
Sara
Do you know Khalid? I saw him at the gym yesterday.
هل تعرفين خالد؟ رأيته في الصالة الرياضية أمس.
Nadia
Yes! I know him well. I used to study with him at university.
نعم! أعرفه جيداً. اعتدت الدراسة معه في الجامعة.
Sara
He looks really fit! Have you spoken to him recently?
يبدو رياضياً جداً! هل تحدثتِ معه مؤخراً؟
Nadia
Not really. I sent him a message last week but he didn't reply.
ليس كثيراً. أرسلت له رسالة الأسبوع الماضي لكنه لم يرد.
Sara
Maybe he's busy. I'll tell him to contact you when I see him!
ربما يكون مشغولاً. سأخبره بالتواصل معك عندما أراه!
Dialogue 02
In the Classroom
Student talking to his teacher about a classmate — طالب يتحدث مع معلمه عن زميله
Omar
Miss, Tariq forgot his book. Can I share mine with him?
أستاذة، نسي طارق كتابه. هل يمكنني مشاركته كتابي؟
Teacher
Yes, of course. Please sit next to him and help him follow the lesson.
نعم، بالطبع. من فضلك اجلس بجانبه وساعده في متابعة الدرس.
Omar
Thank you. Also, Reem has a question. She asked me to tell you about her.
شكراً. أيضاً، ريم لديها سؤال. طلبت مني إخبارك عنها.
Teacher
Of course, tell her to come and see me after class.
بالطبع، أخبرها بأن تأتي وتراني بعد الحصة.
Dialogue 03
At the Shops
Buying a gift for a friend — شراء هدية لصديقة
Layla
I'm looking for a birthday gift for my sister. Can you help me?
أبحث عن هدية عيد ميلاد لأختي. هل يمكنك مساعدتي؟
Shop assistant
Of course! What does she like? Can you tell me more about her?
بالطبع! ماذا تحب؟ هل يمكنك إخباري عنها أكثر؟
Layla
She loves reading. I want to get her something special.
تحب القراءة. أريد أن أحضر لها شيئاً مميزاً.
Shop assistant
How about this book set? Many customers tell us that their friends love it.
ماذا عن هذه المجموعة من الكتب؟ كثير من العملاء يخبروننا أن أصدقاءهم يحبونها.
Layla
Perfect! I'll take it. Can you wrap it for me?
ممتاز! سآخذها. هل يمكنك تغليفها لي؟
Dialogue 04
Talking About Family
Two men talking about their families — رجلان يتحدثان عن عائلتيهما
Tariq
My brother is visiting next week. I haven't seen him in two years!
أخي يزورني الأسبوع القادم. لم أره منذ سنتين!
Khalid
That's great! Are you going to take him anywhere?
رائع! هل ستأخذه إلى أي مكان؟
Tariq
Yes, I want to show him the city. My parents are coming too. I'll pick them up from the airport.
نعم، أريد أن أريه المدينة. والداي آتيان أيضاً. سأستقبلهما من المطار.
Khalid
Lovely! Let me know if you need help. I can drive you all to a good restaurant.
رائع! أخبرني إن احتجت مساعدة. يمكنني اصطحابكم جميعاً إلى مطعم جيد.
Tariq
That's very kind of you! I'll tell them about your offer.
هذا لطيف جداً منك! سأخبرهم بعرضك.
Dialogue 05
Sending a Message
Two friends discussing a group chat — صديقتان تتحدثان عن مجموعة على الهاتف
Reem
Did you see Hana's message in the group? She wants us to meet on Friday.
هل رأيت رسالة هناء في المجموعة؟ تريد منا الاجتماع يوم الجمعة.
Salma
Yes! I replied to her and told her I'm free. What about you?
نعم! رددت عليها وأخبرتها أنني حرة. وأنتِ؟
Reem
I haven't replied yet. I'll send her a message now. Do you know if Sara can join us?
لم أرد بعد. سأرسل لها رسالة الآن. هل تعرفين إن كانت سارة تستطيع الانضمام إلينا؟
Salma
I'm not sure. I'll call her and ask her. She usually replies to me quickly.
لست متأكدة. سأتصل بها وأسألها. هي عادةً ترد علي بسرعة.
Reem
Great! Tell her we miss her. I'll message you later with the plan.
رائع! أخبريها أننا نفتقدها. سأراسلك لاحقاً بالخطة.

📖 Reading — A Letter from Emma

اقرأ النص — ضمائر المفعول به بالخط العريض · رسالة من صديقة مراسلة في لندن

My name is Mariam. I have a pen friend called Emma. She lives in London and she writes to me every month. I love reading her letters because she always describes her family and friends in a funny way.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Emma's Family

In her last letter, Emma told me about her family. Her father is a doctor. Emma admires him very much because he works very hard. Her mother is a teacher. Emma helps her at home every evening. Emma also has a younger brother called Jack. He loves football. Emma watches him play every Saturday. Their dog is called Max. The whole family loves it. Emma takes it for a walk every morning.

👭 Emma's Friends

Emma also wrote about her two best friends, Lily and Sophie. She meets them at school every day. Last week, Lily had a birthday party and Emma invited me — but I live in Jordan, so I couldn't go! Emma sent them both gifts and had a wonderful time. Lily and Sophie always wait for her at the school gate in the morning.

✉️ Emma's Invitation

At the end of the letter, Emma invited me to visit her in London next summer. I am so excited! I told my parents about it and they said yes. I will write to her this week. I will tell her that I can't wait to meet her and her wonderful family!

❓ Comprehension Questions — أسئلة الفهم

اختر الإجابة الصحيحة لكل سؤال

1. Who is Emma?

"I have a pen friend called Emma. She lives in London."

2. Why does Mariam admire Emma's father?

"Emma admires him very much because he works very hard."

3. What does Emma do every Saturday?

"He loves football. Emma watches him play every Saturday."

4. Why couldn't Mariam go to Lily's birthday party?

"Emma invited me — but I live in Jordan, so I couldn't go!"

5. What did Mariam's parents say about the trip to London?

"I told my parents about it and they said yes."

6. In "Lily and Sophie always wait for her at the gate" — who does her refer to?

"Lily and Sophie always wait for her (= Emma) at the school gate."
