Possessive Pronouns

ضمائر الملكية

Learn the difference between possessive adjectives (my, your, his...) and possessive pronouns (mine, yours, his...) — and when to use each one.

⚖️ Possessive Adjective vs Possessive Pronoun
الفرق الأساسي: الصفة الملكية تأتي قبل اسم — الضمير الملكية يأتي بدون اسم
📒 Possessive Adjective
my / your / his / her / its / our / their
تأتي قبل اسم — تصف الاسم
  • This is my book. 🔊
  • Where is your bag? 🔊
  • I like his car. 🔊
  • She forgot her keys. 🔊
  • The cat licked its paw. 🔊
  • We love our school. 🔊
  • They lost their tickets. 🔊
📘 Possessive Pronoun
mine / yours / his / hers / ours / theirs
تأتي وحدها — تحل محل الاسم
  • This book is mine. 🔊
  • Is that bag yours? 🔊
  • That car is his. 🔊
  • The keys are hers. 🔊
  • That school is ours. 🔊
  • The tickets are theirs. 🔊
  • its — ❌ no possessive pronoun form
⚠️ Common Mistakes:
❌ This is my. / This is mine book.
✅ This is mine. / This is my book.

❌ The dog wagged it's tail.  (it's = it is — اختصار)
✅ The dog wagged its tail.  (its = ملكية)
💬 Examples — أمثلة مع الصوت
اضغط على أي مثال للاستماع إليه
1
My name is Sarah. What is your name?
اسمي سارة. ما اسمك؟
2
This is my phone and that one is hers.
هذا هاتفي وذاك هاتفها.
3
Their house is bigger than ours.
بيتهم أكبر من بيتنا.
4
Excuse me, I think this seat is mine.
عفواً، أعتقد أن هذا المقعد لي.
5
The bird hurt its wing.
أصاب الطائر جناحه. (its = للحيوانات والأشياء)
6
Is this pen yours or his?
هل هذا القلم لك أم له؟
7
We painted our room blue. What colour is theirs?
طلينا غرفتنا باللون الأزرق. ما لون غرفتهم؟
8
Her eyes are blue. His are brown.
عيناها زرقاوان. عيناه بنيّتان. (his = ضمير ملكية هنا)
Dialogue 01
Lost & Found 💼
Students find lost items in the classroom
Lena
Excuse me, is this your bag?
عفواً، هل هذه حقيبتك؟
Sara
No, it's not mine. My bag is black. I think it's hers — she left it on her chair.
لا، ليست لي. حقيبتي سوداء. أعتقد أنها لها — تركتها على كرسيها.
Lena
Whose notebook is this? Is it yours?
لمن هذا الدفتر؟ هل هو لك؟
Sara
No, mine is blue. Maybe it's his. Let me ask him!
لا، دفتري أزرق. ربما هو له. دعيني أسأله!
Lena
Omar, is this your notebook?
عمر، هل هذا دفترك؟
Omar
Yes! It's mine! Thank you!
نعم! هو لي! شكراً!
Dialogue 02
Comparing Houses 🏠
Two neighbours talk about their homes
Mrs Ali
Your garden is beautiful!
حديقتك جميلة!
Mrs Nour
Thank you! We planted all the flowers ourselves. Our garden has roses and sunflowers.
شكراً! زرعنا الزهور بأنفسنا. حديقتنا فيها ورد وعباد الشمس.
Mrs Ali
Ours has only grass. My husband wants to grow vegetables in our garden.
حديقتنا فيها عشب فقط. زوجي يريد زراعة خضروات في حديقتنا.
Mrs Nour
Great idea! Their children love playing in their garden every day!
فكرة رائعة! أطفالهم يحبون اللعب في حديقتهم كل يوم!
Dialogue 03
At the Café ☕
Friends argue over who ordered what
Rami
Excuse me, which coffee is mine?
عفواً، أي قهوة لي؟
Waiter
The large one is yours. The small one is his.
الكبيرة لك. الصغيرة له.
Rami
Is this sandwich yours?
هل هذه الشطيرة لك؟
Nadia
No, mine is the cheese one. That must be hers — she ordered tuna.
لا، شطيرتي بالجبن. تلك يجب أن تكون لها — هي طلبت تونة.
Waiter
Her order is tuna, his is chicken. Here you are!
طلبها تونة، طلبه دجاج. تفضلوا!
Dialogue 04
Whose Is It? 🤔
Children ask about objects at home
Ali
Mum, whose jacket is this? Is it yours or Dad's?
أمي، لمن هذه الجاكيت؟ هل هي لك أم لأبي؟
Mum
It's not mine and not his either. Ask your sister — maybe it's hers.
ليست لي ولا له. اسأل أختك — ربما هي لها.
Ali
She said it's not hers. Could it be ours from last year?
قالت ليست لها. هل يمكن أنها لنا من العام الماضي؟
Mum
Wait — our neighbour left his jacket here last week! It must be his!
انتظر — جارنا ترك جاكيته هنا الأسبوع الماضي! يجب أن تكون له!
Dialogue 05
Grammar Class 🎓
Teacher explains possessives with student examples
Teacher
Today we study possessive pronouns. Can you make a sentence with mine?
اليوم ندرس ضمائر الملكية. هل يمكنك بناء جملة بـ mine؟
Student 1
This pencil is mine!
هذا القلم لي!
Teacher
Perfect! Now use yours.
ممتاز! الآن استخدم yours.
Student 2
Is this eraser yours?
هل هذه الممحاة لك؟
Student 3
Her English is better than his. And our classroom is bigger than theirs!
إنجليزيتها أفضل من إنجليزيته. وصفنا أكبر من صفهم!
Teacher
Outstanding! You all understand possessives very well!
رائع جداً! كلكم تفهمون ضمائر الملكية بشكل ممتاز!

🔊 استمع للنص:

Our New Neighbour

Last week, a new family moved into our street. Their house is the white one next to ours. They have two children — a boy and a girl. His name is Adam and hers is Leila.

My mother visited them and brought some food. She said their kitchen is very modern. Their furniture is beautiful too. My sister admired their garden and said, "Ours is much smaller!"

Adam came to play with my brother yesterday. He brought his football. My brother showed him his bicycle. "Is this yours?" Adam asked. "Yes, it's mine," my brother said proudly.

Leila is in my sister's class at school. Her English is very good. My sister said, "Her pronunciation is better than mine!" They are now good friends and do their homework together.

Our parents are happy that the new neighbours are friendly. My father said, "Their children are very polite." I think our street is lucky to have them as our neighbours.

❓ Comprehension Questions — أسئلة الاستيعاب

2. What did Adam bring when he came to play?
3. "Her pronunciation is better than mine!" Who said this?
4. What did the narrator's sister say about their garden?
5. In "His name is Adam and hers is Leila" — what does "hers" replace?
