Present Continuous

المضارع المستمر — ما يحدث الآن وما هو مؤقت

Actions happening right now · Temporary situations · Future plans · Changing trends — and when NOT to use it

📖 Present Continuous — الشرح الكامل

المضارع المستمر — الإيجابي · النفي · السؤال · مقارنة مع المضارع البسيط

I + am + working
You / We / They + are + working
He / She / It + is + working
✍️ القاعدة: am / is / are + الفعل + ing. نستخدمه للتعبير عما يحدث الآن أو في لحظة الكلام. تذكّر: am مع I فقط، are مع You/We/They، is مع He/She/It.
Examples — أمثلة
I am writing an email to my boss.
أنا أكتب بريداً إلكترونياً لمديري.
You are talking too fast. Please slow down.
أنت تتحدث بسرعة كبيرة. تمهّل من فضلك.
We are waiting for the bus.
نحن ننتظر الحافلة.
They are having a meeting right now.
هم في اجتماع الآن.
He is working in the garden.
هو يعمل في الحديقة.
She is reading a really interesting book.
هي تقرأ كتاباً مثيراً للاهتمام.
It is raining outside.
إنها تمطر في الخارج.
My sister is studying for her exam.
أختي تذاكر لامتحانها.
The children are playing in the park.
الأطفال يلعبون في الحديقة.
I am living with my parents at the moment.
أنا أسكن مع والديّ في الوقت الحالي. (مؤقت)
She is learning to drive.
هي تتعلم القيادة.
They are building a new shopping centre near here.
هم يبنون مركز تجاري جديداً قريباً من هنا.
I + am not + working
You / We / They + aren't + working
He / She / It + isn't + working
💡 اختصارات: I'm not — You/We/They aren't — He/She/It isn't. لا توجد اختصار لـ "I am not" — لا نقول "I amn't"!
Examples — أمثلة
I am not working today. I have a day off.
أنا لا أعمل اليوم. لديّ إجازة.
You are not listening to me!
أنت لا تستمع إليّ!
We aren't going to the party tonight.
نحن لن نذهب للحفلة الليلة.
They aren't having lunch yet.
هم لم يتناولوا الغداء بعد.
He isn't driving — he doesn't have a licence.
هو لا يقود — ليس لديه رخصة قيادة.
She isn't sleeping — she's just resting.
هي لا تنام — هي فقط ترتاح.
It isn't raining anymore. The sun is coming out.
لم تعد المطر تهطل. الشمس تظهر.
The baby isn't crying now. She's smiling!
الطفل لا يبكي الآن. هو يبتسم!
I am not wearing a coat — it's warm today!
أنا لا أرتدي معطفاً — الجو دافئ اليوم!
She isn't living in London anymore.
هي لم تعد تسكن في لندن.
We aren't staying at a hotel — we're staying with friends.
نحن لا نقيم في فندق — نقيم عند أصدقاء.
He isn't doing his homework — he's playing games again!
هو لا يذاكر واجبه — هو يلعب الألعاب مرة أخرى!
Am + I + working ?
Are + you / we / they + working ?
Is + he / she / it + working ?
💡 نضع am/is/are في بداية السؤال قبل الفاعل — عكس ترتيب الجملة العادية.

🔑 الفرق الأساسي: المضارع البسيط = عادة دائمة. المضارع المستمر = يحدث الآن / مؤقت.
Present Simple — عادةPresent Continuous — الآن / مؤقت
She works at a bank. (always)She is working from home today. (just today)
I live in Riyadh. (permanent)I am living in a hotel this week. (temporary)
He reads every night. (habit)He is reading a novel. (right now)
It always rains in London. (fact)Look! It is raining! (now)
She teaches at a school. (job)She is teaching online this semester. (now)
I drink coffee every morning. (routine)I am drinking tea today — no coffee! (now)

⚠️ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة

أبرز 10 أخطاء في المضارع المستمر — تعلّمها لتتجنبها

She reading now.
She is reading now.
نسي am/is/are — لا يمكن حذفها في المضارع المستمر
I am work now.
I am working now.
لا بد من إضافة -ing للفعل مع am/is/are
He is swiming.
He is swimming.
swim ينتهي بـ CVC → نضاعف m قبل ing
She is makeing dinner.
She is making dinner.
make ينتهي بـ e صامتة → نحذف e قبل ing
I am knowing the answer.
I know the answer.
know فعل حالة (stative) — لا يُستخدم مع المستمر
Are you working? Yes, I working.
Are you working? Yes, I am.
الإجابة القصيرة: Yes, I am — ليس Yes, I working
What she is doing?
What is she doing?
في أسئلة WH نعكس الترتيب: am/is/are يأتي قبل الفاعل
I am not agree.
I don't agree.
agree فعل حالة → نستخدم المضارع البسيط
She is lieing on the sofa.
She is lying on the sofa.
lie → lie + ing → lie ينتهي بـ ie → نغيّر ie إلى y → lying
He always is forgetting.
He is always forgetting.
always مع to be تأتي بعد is/am/are وليس قبلها

📖 Read the passage — اقرأ النص
It is Sunday morning and the family is having a busy time. The father is cooking breakfast in the kitchen. The mother is talking on the phone. The children are playing in the garden. The dog is sleeping near the door. Tom is not watching TV — he is reading a book. They are all enjoying their Sunday morning.
is / are (مساعد)
فعل + ing
❓ لماذا استخدمنا هذا الشكل؟ — Why this form?
لماذا نستخدم Present Continuous في هذا النص؟
Present Continuous يصف ما يحدث الآن أو في هذه الفترة. كلمات مثل now, right now, at the moment دليل عليه.
في "The father is cooking" — لماذا is وليس are؟
في Present Continuous: is مع he/she/it (مفرد) — are مع we/you/they (جمع).
في "The children are playing" — لماذا are وليس is؟
are تُستخدم مع الجمع. children = أكثر من طفل → جمع → are.
لماذا نكتب having بدون حرف e النهائي؟
have → having, make → making, write → writing. نحذف الـ e الصامتة قبل إضافة ing.
كيف ننفي "He is reading a book"؟
لننفي Present Continuous نضيف not بعد is/are/am. الفعل يبقى بصيغة ing.

know · love · hate · like · want · need · understand · believe · agree · see · hear · own

✅ She loves it.

📚 A Monday Morning in the City

It is Monday morning and the city is waking up. The streets are getting busy. People are rushing to work — some are walking fast, others are driving, and many are checking their phones as they go.

In a coffee shop on the main street, the barista Emma is making drinks. She is smiling and is talking to customers, even though she is working her second job this week. A man in a suit is sitting by the window. He is reading a newspaper and is drinking an espresso. He isn't looking happy — he is probably thinking about his 9 o'clock meeting.

Outside, two teenagers are waiting for the bus. They are both wearing headphones and aren't talking to each other. The bus is running late — as usual.

At the hospital across the street, doctors and nurses are starting their morning shifts. Dr Khan is examining a patient and Nurse Li is writing notes on a tablet. In the waiting room, an old man is resting with his eyes closed and a young woman is flipping through a magazine.

Meanwhile, in a school nearby, children are arriving for the day. Some are running into the playground, full of energy. Others are walking very slowly — clearly not excited about Mondays. The teacher Mr Evans is standing at the gate and is greeting everyone with a cheerful "Good morning!"

The city is alive. Life is moving. And somewhere, someone is definitely missing their alarm and is running very, very late.
