Third Conditional & Regrets

الشرط الثالث والندم

Use the Third Conditional to imagine how the past could have been different, and master four key regret structures — wish / if only / should have / could have — to talk about things you wish you had (or hadn't) done.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
🟣 What is the Third Conditional?
ما هو الشرط الثالث؟
⏰ Imaginary past — it did NOT happen
If + had + past participle  ,  would have + past participle
• We imagine a different result for something that happened (or didn't happen) in the past.
• The condition is impossible to change — it's history.
• Often expresses regret, criticism, or speculation about the past.
• "If I had studied harder, I would have passed the exam." (but I didn't study — so I didn't pass) 🔉
• "If she had left earlier, she would have caught the train."
• "If we hadn't argued, he wouldn't have left."
📋 Formation — All Forms
التكوين الكامل — إيجابي / نفي / سؤال
Third Conditional
✅ Positive
If + subject + had + past participle → subject + would have + past participle
If he had apologised, she would have forgiven him. 🔉
❌ Negative
If + subject + hadn't + past participle → subject + wouldn't have + past participle
If I hadn't stayed up late, I wouldn't have been so tired. 🔉
? Question
Would + subject + have + past participle (if + past perfect)?
Would you have taken the job if they had offered it? 🔉
💡 Contraction tip: "If I 'd known…" = "If I had known…"  —  the short form is very common in spoken English.
🔄 2nd Conditional vs 3rd Conditional
الفرق بين الشرط الثاني والثالث
Feature 🔵 2nd Conditional 🟣 3rd Conditional
Time Present / Future (imaginary) Past (impossible to change)
If clause If + Past Simple If + Past Perfect (had done)
Result clause would + verb would have + past participle
Example If I had more money, I would buy a car. 🔉 If I had had more money, I would have bought a car. 🔉
Meaning Unlikely but still possible Impossible — it already happened differently
😭 Regret Structures — أساليب الندم
أربع طرق مختلفة للتعبير عن الندم على الماضي
🟣 Soft regret
wish + past perfect
wish + had + past participle
A personal regret about something that happened (or didn't happen) in the past
"I wish I had studied medicine." 🔉
"She wishes she hadn't said that."
🔴 Strong regret / frustration
if only + past perfect
if only + had + past participle
Stronger than "wish" — expresses frustration or deep regret about the past
"If only I had listened to my father!" 🔉
"If only we hadn't missed the flight!"
🔵 Regret / criticism
should have + past participle
should have + past participle
An action was the right thing to do but wasn't done — or the opposite
"I should have saved my work." 🔉
"You shouldn't have been so rude."
🟢 Unrealised possibility
could have + past participle
could have + past participle
Something was possible in the past but didn't happen
"I could have helped you — why didn't you ask?" 🔉
"She could have been a great doctor."
📊 Regret Intensity Scale
wish
general regret — common, neutral
if only
stronger — frustration, deep emotion
🟡 would have — alone for speculation
would have بدون شرط — للتخمين عن الماضي
"would have" can also appear without an if-clause to speculate about what might have happened:

• "I would have called you, but my phone was dead." 🔉
• "She would have been so happy to hear that." 🔉
• "They would have won if the referee hadn't made that mistake." 🔉
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
If I would have known, I would have helped. → If I had known, I would have helped.
جملة الشرط تبدأ بـ had — ليس would have
❌
I wish I studied harder. (for past regret) → I wish I had studied harder.
الندم على الماضي يحتاج past perfect بعد wish
❌
You should have to tell me. → You should have told me.
should have لا تتبعها "to" — مباشرةً past participle
❌
If I had known, I will have called. → If I had known, I would have called.
النتيجة دائماً would have — ليس will have
❌
If only I listened. (for past) → If only I had listened!
if only للماضي تحتاج past perfect — ليس past simple
💡 Quick Tips
No would in if
NEVER write "would have" in the if-clause: ✅ "If I had known…" 🔉الـ would have تأتي في النتيجة فقط — ليس في جملة if
if only = stronger wish
"If only" = emotional version of "wish" — use it when feeling strong regret or frustration 🔉if only = wish بمشاعر أقوى وندم أعمق
'd = had
"If I'd known" = "If I had known" — contraction is very natural in speech 🔉'd اختصار لـ had في الشرط الثالث — شائعة جداً في المحادثة
should vs could
should have = it was the right thing to do  |  could have = it was possible but didn't happen 🔉should have = الشيء الصحيح كان كذا | could have = كان ممكناً

📘 The Letter Never Sent
الرسالة التي لم تُرسَل

Khalid found the old letter while clearing his father's desk. It was addressed to him, dated fifteen years ago, and never sent. 🔉

His father had written about the family business. If Khalid had joined the company after university, it would have survived. Instead, Khalid had moved abroad to pursue a career in music. 🔉

Reading the words now, Khalid felt a wave of regret. He wished he had read this letter earlier. If only his father had sent it. 🔉

He should have spent more time talking to his father about the business. He could have helped — even from abroad. 🔉

The business had eventually closed. If they had communicated better, so many things would have been different. 🔉

Khalid folded the letter carefully and put it in his pocket. He would have given anything to go back and make different choices. But some things, once lost, cannot be recovered. 🔉
