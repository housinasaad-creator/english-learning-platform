🧩 Nationality Suffixes — لاحقات الجنسية
كيف تُكوَّن كلمة الجنسية من اسم الدولة — لاحظ النمط!
-ian / -an
Jordan → Jordanian
Egypt → Egyptian
Brazil → Brazilian
Canada → Canadian
Australia → Australian
-ish
England → English
Spain → Spanish
Turkey → Turkish
Sweden → Swedish
-ese
China → Chinese
Japan → Japanese
Portugal → Portuguese
Same / Irregular
France → French
Germany → German
Italy → Italian
Russia → Russian
⚠️ الجنسية تُكتب دائماً بحرف كبير في الإنجليزية: French, Japanese, Jordanian — وليس french أو japanese.
💬 Key Patterns — الجمل الأساسية
كيف تسأل وتجيب عن الجنسية والدولة
Asking
Where are you from?
→
I'm from Jordan.
Asking
What nationality are you?
→
I'm Jordanian.
Yes/No
Are you French?
→
Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. I'm Italian.
3rd Person
Where is she from?
→
She's from Japan. She's Japanese.
Language
What language do you speak?
→
I speak Arabic. / My language is Arabic.
Adjective
Nationality + Noun
→
a French student / an Egyptian doctor
💡 Remember: Nationality adjectives always go BEFORE the noun.
✅ a Japanese car    ❌ a car Japanese
الصفة تأتي قبل الاسم دائماً في الإنجليزية — عكس العربية.
📝 Examples — أمثلة
She is from Brazil. She is Brazilian.
هي من البرازيل. هي برازيلية.
They are from Germany. They speak German.
هم من ألمانيا. يتحدثون الألمانية.
He is an Australian teacher.
هو معلم أسترالي.
I am not Spanish. I am Italian.
لست إسبانياً. أنا إيطالي.
What nationality is she? She is Canadian.
ما جنسيتها؟ هي كندية.
Dialogue 01
Where Are You From? 🌍
Two new students meet on the first day
Omar
Hi! My name is Omar. I'm from Jordan.
مرحباً! اسمي عمر. أنا من الأردن.
Lena
Nice to meet you, Omar! I'm Lena. I'm from Germany. I'm German.
يسعدني لقاؤك يا عمر! أنا لينا. أنا من ألمانيا. أنا ألمانية.
Omar
Oh really? Do you speak German?
حقاً؟ هل تتحدثين الألمانية؟
Lena
Yes, I do! And I speak a little English.
نعم! وأتحدث القليل من الإنجليزية.
Omar
Your English is very good!
إنجليزيتكِ جيدة جداً!
Lena
Thank you! What language do you speak?
شكراً! ما اللغة التي تتحدثها؟
Omar
I speak Arabic. It's my first language.
أتحدث العربية. إنها لغتي الأولى.

Food & Nationality 🍜
Talking about food from different countries
Mia
I love this food! What is it?
أحب هذا الطعام! ما هو؟
Yuki
It's sushi! It's Japanese food.
إنه سوشي! إنه طعام ياباني.
Mia
Oh, is it from China?
أوه، هل هو من الصين؟

No, it's not! Sushi is from Japan. Japanese food is delicious!
لا، ليس كذلك! السوشي من اليابان. الطعام الياباني لذيذ!

A World Map in the Classroom

Mr. Davis is an English teacher. Every year, he puts a big world map on the classroom wall. He asks every student to put a pin on their country.

This year, the class has students from many places. Omar is from Jordan. He puts his pin on the Middle East. Lena is German. She puts her pin on Germany in Europe. Yuki is from Japan. She puts her pin on Asia.

Carlos is Spanish. He puts his pin on Spain. Hana is Brazilian. Brazil is in South America. Hana loves her country. She says, "Brazil is big and beautiful!"

Mr. Davis looks at the map and smiles. "We have students from five continents!" he says. "Jordan is in Asia. Germany and Spain are in Europe. Brazil is in South America."

"This is wonderful," says Omar. "We are all different nationalities, but we are one class!"

Everyone agrees and claps. Mr. Davis says, "This is why English is important — it connects the world!"
