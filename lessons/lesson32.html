Quantifiers

محددات الكمية

Master words that express how much or how many — including many / much, few / little, some / any, a lot of / plenty of, less / fewer, and more — with clear rules for countable and uncountable nouns.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
📌 Countable vs Uncountable Nouns — أسماء معدودة وغير معدودة
قبل تعلم المحددات، يجب معرفة نوع الاسم — لأن المحدد يختلف بحسب النوع
🔵 Countable — معدود
يمكن عدّه — له مفرد وجمع
book / books student / students car / cars idea / ideas chair / chairs question / questions
🟣 Uncountable — غير معدود
لا يُعَدّ — لا جمع له — لا نضع a/an قبله
water money information advice furniture news time knowledge traffic homework
📊 Quantifiers Overview — جدول المحددات الكامل
كل محدد مع النوع الذي يستخدم معه — معدود 🔵 أو غير معدود 🟣 أو كلاهما 🟢
Quantifier 🔵 Countable 🟣 Uncountable 🟢 Both
many many students / many seats 🔉 — —
much — much water / much time 🔉 —
a few a few friends (= some) 🔉 — —
few few friends (= not enough) 🔉 — —
a little — a little milk (= some) 🔉 —
little — little hope (= not enough) 🔉 —
several several times / several people 🔉 — —
fewer fewer students / fewer cars 🔉 — —
less — less sugar / less time 🔉 —
some / any — — some/any water, some/any books 🔉
a lot of — — a lot of friends, a lot of time 🔉
plenty of — — plenty of time, plenty of seats 🔉
enough — — enough chairs, enough food 🔉
no / not any — — no time, no friends 🔉
🔍 few / little vs a few / a little — الفرق الدقيق
الفرق بين وجود الكلمة a يُغيّر المعنى من سلبي إلى إيجابي
🔴 few = NOT ENOUGH
few
عدد قليل جداً — يشعر بالسلبية
+ countable plural
"I have few friends here." (I feel lonely 😟)
🟢 a few = SOME (okay)
a few
بعض — يكفي — شعور إيجابي
+ countable plural
"I have a few friends here." (I'm okay 😊)
🔴 little = NOT ENOUGH
little
كمية قليلة جداً — يشعر بالسلبية
+ uncountable
"There is little hope." (the situation is bad 😟)
🟢 a little = SOME (okay)
a little
بعض الشيء — يكفي — شعور إيجابي
+ uncountable
"There is a little milk." (we're fine 😊)
🔄 some vs any — متى نستخدم كل منهما؟
كلاهما يُستخدم مع المعدود والغير معدود — لكن السياق يحدد الاختيار
some
  • ➕ Affirmative sentences
    I have some money. / She bought some books.
  • 🙏 Offers / Requests
    Would you like some tea? / Can I have some water?
any
  • ❌ Negative sentences
    I don't have any money. / There isn't any milk.
  • ❓ Questions (genuine)
    Do you have any questions? / Is there any sugar?
  • ✅ "It doesn't matter which"
    Take any seat you like!
Trick: في العروض والطلبات نستخدم some حتى لو كانت جملة سؤال:
"Would you like some coffee?" ✅  (offer — نتوقع نعم)
"Do you have any coffee?" ✅  (genuine question — لا نعرف الإجابة)
📉 less vs fewer — خطأ شائع جداً
fewer للمعدود (يُعَدّ) — less لغير المعدود (لا يُعَدّ)
less
+ uncountable nouns — أسماء غير معدودة
less water  |  less time  |  less money  |  less traffic
fewer
+ countable nouns (plural) — أسماء معدودة
fewer students  |  fewer cars  |  fewer mistakes  |  fewer people
❓ many vs much — في الأسئلة والنفي
many للمعدود — much لغير المعدود — في الأسئلة والنفي بشكل رئيسي
🔵 many + countable
many
أسئلة ونفي — مع المعدود الجمع
How many students?  |  not many seats 🔉
🟣 much + uncountable
much
أسئلة ونفي — مع غير المعدود
How much water?  |  not much time 🔉
Tip: في الجمل الإيجابية — نستخدم a lot of بدل much/many لأنها أكثر طبيعية:
❌ "I have much money." (formal/odd)    ✅ "I have a lot of money." (natural)
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
هذه الأخطاء يقع فيها حتى المتعلمون المتقدمون
❌
much friends  →  many friends
friends معدود ← much فقط لغير المعدود
❌
less students  →  fewer students
students معدود ← نستخدم fewer وليس less
❌
a few money  →  a little money
money غير معدود ← a few فقط للمعدود
❌
many informations  →  a lot of information
information غير معدود — لا يُجمع — نستخدم a lot of
❌
an advice  →  a piece of advice / some advice
advice غير معدود — لا يُستخدم معه a/an
❌
I have much free time today.  →  I have a lot of free time today.
في الجمل الإيجابية: a lot of أكثر طبيعية من much
💡 Quick Tips
enough
enough + noun: "enough time" ✅  |  adj + enough: "good enough" ✅ 🔉enough قبل الاسم — بعد الصفة
plenty of
plenty of = more than enough (يكفي وزيادة) — "We have plenty of time." 🔉plenty of تعني وفرة — ليس نقصاً ولا مجرد كفاية
no = not any
"There is no milk." = "There isn't any milk." — same meaning 🔉no ونفي + any يؤديان نفس المعنى — no أقوى وأكثر إيجازاً
several
several = more than two but not very many — "I've been there several times." 🔉several = أكثر من اثنين، أقل من كثير — للمعدود فقط

📘 Feeding a City
إطعام مدينة

Every day, a large city needs an enormous amount of food to feed its people. 🔉

There is plenty of food available in supermarkets, but not all families have enough money to buy what they need. 🔉

Many charities work hard to make sure that few people go to bed hungry. 🔉

However, there is still a lot of food waste. Several studies have shown that supermarkets throw away less food than households do. 🔉

Experts say we need fewer single-use plastic packages and a little more creativity in using leftovers. 🔉

There is no simple solution, but with some effort from every household, a great deal of waste can be prevented. 🔉
