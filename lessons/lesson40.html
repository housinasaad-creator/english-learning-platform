Idioms & Collocations

المصطلحات والتعبيرات

Sound like a native speaker by mastering two key areas: idioms — fixed expressions whose meaning you can't guess from the words — and collocations — word pairs that naturally belong together. Both are essential for fluent, natural English.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
💬 Part 1
Idioms — التعبيرات الاصطلاحية
تعبيرات ثابتة لا تُفهم من الكلمات المفردة
An idiom is a group of words whose combined meaning is different from the literal meaning of each word.

• "Break a leg!" ≠ break your actual leg  →  it means Good luck!
• "It cost an arm and a leg." ≠ body parts  →  it was very expensive.

You must learn idioms as fixed units — you cannot change the words or guess the meaning.
🦴 BodyBody Part Idioms — تعبيرات أعضاء الجسم
break a leg 🔉
= Good luck!
بالتوفيق! (يُقال قبل عرض أو مقابلة)
"Your interview is today? Break a leg!"
cost an arm and a leg 🔉
= very expensive
غالي جداً / بثمن باهظ
"That new laptop cost an arm and a leg."
pull someone's leg 🔉
= to joke / tease
يمزح / يسخر من شخص بشكل ودي
"Are you serious or just pulling my leg?"
get cold feet 🔉
= become nervous / lose confidence
يتردد / يخاف في اللحظة الأخيرة
"He got cold feet before the wedding."
keep an eye on 🔉
= watch carefully / monitor
يراقب / يبقي عينه على شيء
"Can you keep an eye on my bag?"
turn a blind eye 🔉
= deliberately ignore
يتجاهل عن قصد / يغض الطرف
"Management turned a blind eye to the problem."
⏰ TimeTime & Situation Idioms — تعبيرات الوقت والمواقف
once in a blue moon 🔉
= very rarely
نادراً جداً / بالكاد
"She calls me once in a blue moon."
in the nick of time 🔉
= just in time / at the last moment
في اللحظة المناسبة / قبل فوات الأوان
"The ambulance arrived in the nick of time."
beat around the bush 🔉
= avoid the main point / be indirect
يتحاشى الموضوع / يدور حوله دون أن يصل
"Stop beating around the bush — what do you want?"
kill two birds with one stone 🔉
= achieve two things with one action
يصيب عصفورين بحجر واحد / يجمع فائدتين
"I'll shop on the way to the gym — kill two birds with one stone."
💪 EffortEffort & Success Idioms — تعبيرات الجهد والنجاح
hit the nail on the head 🔉
= say exactly the right thing
يصيب كبد الحقيقة / يتكلم بدقة تامة
"Your analysis hit the nail on the head."
bite off more than you can chew 🔉
= take on too much / overcommit
يأخذ على عاتقه أكثر مما يستطيع
"She bit off more than she could chew with three projects at once."
go the extra mile 🔉
= do more than expected
يبذل مجهوداً إضافياً / يتجاوز المطلوب
"She always goes the extra mile for her students."
bite the bullet 🔉
= endure pain / face difficulty bravely
يتحمل الأمر / يواجه الوضع الصعب بشجاعة
"Just bite the bullet and tell him the truth."
the ball is in your court 🔉
= it's your turn / your decision to make
القرار بيدك / دورك أنت الآن

burn bridges 🔉
= permanently damage relationships / close future options
يقطع العلاقات بشكل نهائي / يغلق الأبواب
"Don't burn bridges when you quit — you might need them later."
📝 Part 2
Collocations — التلازم اللغوي
كلمتان أو أكثر تُستخدمان معاً بشكل طبيعي في اللغة
A collocation is a pair (or group) of words that naturally go together in English. Native speakers use these automatically — learners must memorise them as chunks.

• We say make a mistake — NOT do a mistake
• We say pass an exam — NOT win an exam
• We say pay attention — NOT give attention
📋 Key Verb Collocations
أفعال رئيسية مع مصاحباتها الطبيعية
make 🔉
a decision a mistake progress an effort a difference money a plan an appointment a phone call
do 🔉
homework damage your best research a favour business exercise the dishes well
take 🔉
a break a risk an opportunity notes a photo part in time responsibility
have 🔉
a rest a meeting an argument a look a shower second thoughts a conversation fun
get 🔉
a job permission lost married tired started in touch rid of
⚠ Tricky 🔉
commit a crime pass an exam miss an opportunity waste time pay attention cause damage

⚠ Common Collocation Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
I did a mistake. → I made a mistake.
mistake تلازم مع make — وليس do
❌
She won the exam. → She passed the exam.
الامتحان يُجتاز (pass) — ليس يُفوز (win)
❌
He lost time on that project. → He wasted time on that project.
الوقت يُهدر (waste) — ليس يُضاع (lose)
❌
Give attention to the instructions. → Pay attention to the instructions.
الانتباه يُعطى بـ pay — ليس give
❌
I did a photo of the view. → I took a photo of the view.
الصورة تُلتقط (take) — ليس do
❌
He made a crime. → He committed a crime.
الجريمة تُرتكب (commit) — ليس make
💡 Quick Tips
Idioms
Never translate idioms word-by-word — learn the whole phrase + its meaning as one unit 🔉لا تترجم الـ idioms كلمة كلمة — احفظ التعبير كوحدة واحدة مع معناه
Collocations
When you learn a new noun, always note its collocating verb: make a decision, pass an exam, take a break 🔉تعلّم الاسم مع الفعل الذي يصاحبه دائماً
make vs do
make = create / produce something  |  do = perform an activity/task — but always check! 🔉make = تصنع شيئاً | do = تؤدي نشاطاً — لكن راجع دائماً
Register
Idioms are mostly informal — great in speech and casual writing, but avoid them in formal academic texts 🔉الـ idioms عامية في الغالب — مناسبة للحديث والكتابة غير الرسمية

📘 Starting Over
البداية من جديد

After fifteen years in the same company, Rania finally made the decision to leave and pursue her dream of opening her own business. 🔉

Her friends thought she was biting off more than she could chew. "You'll burn bridges with the company," one warned. "They'll never take you back." 🔉

But Rania had done her research. She had saved money, made a solid plan, and even taken a business course the previous year. She wasn't going to waste any more time. 🔉

The first few months were difficult. The rent for her shop cost an arm and a leg, and making progress felt slow. There were days when she got cold feet and considered going back to her old job. 🔉

But she kept an eye on her goals and always went the extra mile for her customers — staying late, offering personalised advice, and paying attention to every small detail. 🔉

In the nick of time, just before she ran out of savings, a major client discovered her shop and placed a huge order. Rania had hit the nail on the head — her quality and dedication were exactly what the market needed. 🔉
