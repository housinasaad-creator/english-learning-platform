Narrative Tenses

أزمنة السرد والماضي التام

Master storytelling in English using three past tenses together: Past Simple for main events, Past Continuous for background actions, and Past Perfect for actions that happened before the story began — plus the key connectors that link them all.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
📌 The Three Narrative Tenses — أزمنة السرد الثلاثة
عند رواية قصة في الماضي، نستخدم ثلاثة أزمنة معاً — لكل واحد دور مختلف
⏪ Earlier action
Past Perfect
الماضي التام — ما حدث قبل
had + past participle
Actions that happened before the main story events
"She had already left when I arrived." 🔉
🎨 Background
Past Continuous
الماضي المستمر — الخلفية والسياق
was / were + verb-ing
Background scenes, atmosphere, or interrupted actions
"It was raining when we left." 🔉
▶ Main events
Past Simple
الماضي البسيط — أحداث القصة الرئيسية
verb-ed / irregular
The main sequence of events — what happened, step by step
"She arrived, opened the door and sat down." 🔉
📅 Timeline — خط الزمن
تخيّل خطاً زمنياً: الماضي التام يأتي أولاً — ثم الأحداث الرئيسية — والمستمر يستمر في الخلفية
Story Timeline
Past Perfect
had eaten
Past Continuous
was raining
Past Simple
arrived
"When I arrived at the station, it was raining heavily. My friend had already bought the tickets, so we went straight to the platform." 🔉
Past Perfect = before the story
Past Continuous = background
Past Simple = main events
🟣 Past Perfect — التكوين والاستخدام
الماضي التام = had + التصريف الثالث (Past Participle) — لجميع الضمائر
Formation — التكوين
➕ Affirmative
Subject + had + Past Participle
She had finished her work before dinner. 🔉
❌ Negative
Subject + hadn't + Past Participle
He hadn't eaten anything all day. 🔉
❓ Question
Had + Subject + Past Participle?
Had you met her before the party? 🔉
📍 Short forms
I'd / she'd / they'd + PP
I'd already seen that film twice. 🔉
Key uses of Past Perfect:
• Action completed before another past action: "When she arrived, he had left."
• To give background reason: "He was tired because he hadn't slept."
• With by the time: "By the time we got there, the film had started."
• With already / just / never / yet: "I had never seen anything like it."
🔄 Past Simple vs Past Perfect — متى أستخدم أيّهما؟
السؤال الجوهري: هل الحدثان في نفس التسلسل؟ أم أحدهما سبق الآخر بوضوح؟
Situation 🟢 Past Simple 🟣 Past Perfect
Sequence of events She ate dinner. Then she watched TV. 🔉 She had eaten dinner before she watched TV. 🔉
Arrival + earlier action I arrived and then she left. 🔉 When I arrived, she had already left. 🔉
Reason / cause He was tired. He didn't sleep. 🔉 He was tired because he hadn't slept. 🔉
By the time We got there. The film started. 🔉 By the time we got there, the film had started. 🔉
First experience She visited Paris last year. 🔉 She had never visited Paris before that trip. 🔉
🔵 Past Continuous in Narratives — الماضي المستمر في السرد
يرسم الماضي المستمر "صورة الخلفية" للقصة — ما كان يحدث عندما بدأت الأحداث الرئيسية
Three roles in storytelling
🎨
Background / setting the scene — ما كان يحدث في الخلفية
"The sun was shining and birds were singing when we set off." 🔉
⚡
Interrupted action — فعل مستمر قُطع بحدث مفاجئ
"I was walking to the shop when I suddenly heard a loud noise." 🔉
🔁
Simultaneous actions — فعلان يحدثان في نفس الوقت
"She was cooking while he was setting the table." 🔉
💡 when vs while — الفرق المهم
خطأ شائع جداً — when وwhile يؤديان أدواراً مختلفة في الجمل السردية
when
+ short / completed action (Past Simple)
= at the moment that
"When I arrived, she was cooking." ✅
"When he called, I was sleeping." ✅ 🔉
while
+ longer / ongoing action (Past Continuous)
= during the time that
"While I was sleeping, he called." ✅
"While she was reading, the phone rang." ✅ 🔉
⚠ "When I was cooking, the phone rang." ✅   (when can also introduce a continuous action)
❌ "While I arrived, she was cooking." ❌   (while cannot introduce a completed/sudden action)
🔗 Narrative Connectors — أدوات الربط السردي
هذه الكلمات تربط أحداث القصة وتُوضّح التسلسل الزمني
🟢 Sequence
first · then · after that · next · finally
التسلسل: أولاً، ثم، بعد ذلك، أخيراً 🔉
🔵 Time
when · while · as · as soon as · just as
وقت الحدث: عندما، بينما، حالما 🔉
🟣 Before/After
before · after · by the time · until · once
قبل/بعد: قبل أن، بعد أن، بحلول الوقت 🔉
🟡 Contrast
however · but · although · even though
التناقض: لكن، بالرغم من، رغم أن 🔉
🟠 Result
so · therefore · as a result · consequently
النتيجة: لذلك، ونتيجةً لذلك 🔉
🔴 Sudden
suddenly · all of a sudden · out of nowhere
المفاجأة: فجأة، على حين غرة 🔉
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
When I arrived, she left already. → When I arrived, she had already left.
الحدث الذي جاء قبل وصولي → يجب استخدام الماضي التام
❌
While I arrived, she was cooking. → When I arrived, she was cooking.
arrive فعل لحظي — يأتي مع when وليس while
❌
He was eating and then he left. → He ate and then he left.
الأحداث المتسلسلة المنتهية تستخدم الماضي البسيط وليس المستمر
❌
I had seen him yesterday. → I saw him yesterday.
مع ظروف زمن محددة (yesterday/last week) → الماضي البسيط وليس التام
❌
By the time I got there, the film started. → By the time I got there, the film had started.
"by the time" يستلزم الماضي التام للحدث الأول
💡 Quick Tips
Order test
With two past events, always ask: which one happened first? → that one gets past perfect. 🔉عند ذكر حدثين ماضيين — الأقدم يأخذ الماضي التام
after / before
With after and before, past perfect is optional — the connector already shows the order. 🔉مع after وbefore، الماضي التام اختياري لأن الترتيب واضح من الكلمة نفسها
already/just/never
already, just, never, yet → strong signals for past perfect in narrative. 🔉هذه الكلمات تشير بقوة إلى وجوب استخدام الماضي التام
suddenly
suddenly always introduces a Past Simple event — it's a sudden, completed action. 🔉suddenly تُقدّم دائماً فعلاً ماضياً بسيطاً — لأنها لحظية مفاجئة

📘 The Missing Painting
اللوحة المفقودة

Detective Karim arrived at the gallery early on Monday morning. The staff were standing outside looking worried. 🔉

Someone had stolen the most valuable painting in the collection during the night. The glass case was broken and the alarm was still flashing. 🔉

Karim examined the room carefully. He noticed that the thief had entered through a small window at the back. The window had been left unlocked. 🔉

While Karim was looking at the CCTV footage, his assistant found a glove near the window. Someone had dropped it while climbing through. 🔉

By the time Karim had reviewed all the evidence, he was certain he knew who the thief was. He had seen that face before — it was someone who had worked at the gallery two years earlier. 🔉

He called the police immediately and within an hour, they had found the painting. It was hidden in a warehouse just outside the city. 🔉
