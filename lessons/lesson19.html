Present Perfect

مقدمة المضارع التام

Discover how to talk about life experiences, recent events, and actions that connect the past to the present.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
💡 What is the Present Perfect? — ما هو المضارع التام؟
نستخدم المضارع التام للحديث عن أحداث في الماضي لها صلة بالحاضر — ماذا جربت في حياتك؟ ماذا فعلت للتو؟ كم من الوقت أمضيت؟
Subject + have / has + Past Participle
Subject + have / has + Past Participle
I / We / You / They → have + past participle  |  He / She / It → has + past participle
1I have visited Paris.لقد زرت باريس.🔉
2She has eaten sushi before.لقد أكلت السوشي من قبل.🔉
3They have never seen snow.لم يروا الثلج قط.🔉
🔁 have vs has — متى نستخدم have أو has؟
اختيار have أو has يعتمد على الضمير (الفاعل) في الجملة
I  ·  You  ·  We  ·  They
have
أنا / أنت / نحن / هم
He  ·  She  ·  It
has
هو / هي / هو (شيء)
1We have finished the project.لقد أنهينا المشروع.🔉
2He has read the report.لقد قرأ التقرير.🔉
3You have done a great job.لقد أديت عملاً رائعاً.🔉

🌍 Life Experience — التجربة
ever never before
Have you ever tried Thai food? 🔉
I have never been to Japan. 🔉
للتعبير عن تجربة (أو عدمها) في الحياة
⚡ Recent Action — حدث للتو
just
I have just finished my homework. 🔉
She has just called. 🔉
للتعبير عن حدث وقع للتو (منذ قليل)
⏳ Unfinished — مستمر حتى الآن
for since
I have lived here for five years. 🔉
She has worked here since 2019. 🔉
for + مدة / since + نقطة زمنية
✅ Already / Yet
already yet
I have already eaten. (+) 🔉
Have you packed yet? (?) 🔉
already في الإيجاب / yet في السؤال والنفي
for = مدة زمنية  ·  for two years / for a long time / for three months
since = نقطة زمنية  ·  since 2020 / since Monday / since I was a child
❌ Negative & Questions — النفي والاستفهام
لتحويل الجملة للنفي نضيف not بعد have/has — وللسؤال نقدم have/has على الفاعل
❌ Negative
Subject + haven't / hasn't + PP
I haven't seen that film. 🔉
She hasn't finished yet. 🔉
They haven't met before. 🔉
❓ Question
Have / Has + Subject + PP?
Have you ever been to London? 🔉
Has she called you? 🔉
Have they arrived yet? 🔉
Short answers: Yes, I have.  /  No, I haven't.  /  Yes, she has.  /  No, she hasn't.

📘 My Life So Far
قصيدة حياتي حتى الآن

My name is Karim and I have had an interesting life so far. 🔉

I have visited eight countries and I have tasted food from all over the world. 🔉

I have lived in this city since I was eighteen years old. 🔉

I have worked as a teacher for twelve years. 🔉

I have written two books about English grammar and both have been very popular. 🔉

I have always loved languages. I have already learned three: Arabic, English and French. 🔉

I have never regretted choosing this path. 🔉

I have just started learning Spanish. It's never too late! 🔉
