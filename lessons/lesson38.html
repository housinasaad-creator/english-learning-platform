Advanced Passive

المبني للمجهول المتقدم

Go beyond the basic passive and master five advanced structures: modal passive, perfect passive, continuous passive, reporting passive (it is said / he is believed to be), and the informal get passive — plus the key difference between by and with.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
🔄 Quick Review — Basic Passive (Lesson 28)
مراجعة سريعة — المبني للمجهول الأساسي
🧱 Basic passive = be + past participle
Form
be + past participle
am/is/are/was/were + pp
Present
Coffee is grown in Brazil. 🔉
Past
The letter was sent yesterday. 🔉
Why use it?
Agent unknown, unimportant, or for formal/objective tone
🧠 Five Advanced Passive Types
خمسة أنواع متقدمة من المبني للمجهول
🟣 Type 1
Modal Passive
modal + be + past participle
"This can be solved." 🔉
"The report must be submitted by Friday."
"Mistakes could be avoided."
"The package will be delivered tomorrow."
🟠 Type 2
Perfect Passive
have/has/had been + past participle
"The contract has been signed." 🔉
"The letter had been sent before he arrived."
"By Monday, it will have been completed."
🔵 Type 3
Continuous Passive
is/are/was/were being + past participle
"The road is being repaired." 🔉
"The suspect was being questioned."
"New schools are being built in the city."
🟡 Type 4
Reporting Passive
It is said/believed that… / He is said to…
"It is believed that she is the best." 🔉
"She is believed to be the best."
"It is reported that prices will rise."
🟢 Type 5 — Informal
Get Passive
get + past participle
"She got promoted last month." 🔉
"He got arrested at the airport."
"I got confused by the instructions."
"They got married in Paris."
📋 Passive in All Tenses — Master Table
المبني للمجهول في كل الأزمنة — جدول شامل
TenseFormExample
Present Simpleis/are + ppEnglish is spoken here. 🔉
Past Simplewas/were + ppThe window was broken. 🔉
Present Continuousis/are being + ppThe road is being repaired. 🔉
Past Continuouswas/were being + ppThe car was being towed. 🔉
Present Perfecthas/have been + ppThe contract has been signed. 🔉
Past Perfecthad been + ppThe letter had been sent already. 🔉
Future (will)will be + ppResults will be announced tomorrow. 🔉
Future Perfectwill have been + ppBy Monday, it will have been completed. 🔉
Modalmodal + be + ppThis must be done today. 🔉
📰 Reporting Passive — المجهول في الأخبار والتقارير
يُستخدم كثيراً في الأخبار والكتابة الرسمية لتجنب ذكر المصدر
Two patterns — both mean the same thing:

• It is [verb] that + clause
• Subject + is [verb] to + infinitive
VerbIt is… that…Subject is… to…
say It is said that he is very wealthy. 🔉 He is said to be very wealthy. 🔉
believe It is believed that the company is profitable. 🔉 The company is believed to be profitable. 🔉
report It is reported that prices will rise. 🔉 Prices are reported to be rising. 🔉
think It is thought that he left the country. 🔉 He is thought to have left the country. 🔉
expect It is expected that the law will change. 🔉 The law is expected to change. 🔉
know It is known that she is an expert. 🔉 She is known to be an expert. 🔉
🧭 by vs with in Passive Sentences
الفرق بين by و with في جمل المجهول
by
Introduces the agent — the person or thing that performed the action
"The novel was written by Hemingway." 🔉
"The window was broken by a child."
"The building was designed by a famous architect."
with
Introduces the instrument / tool used to perform the action
"The door was opened with a key." 🔉
"The letter was written with a pen."
"The sculpture was carved with a chisel."
• Both can appear together: "The window was broken with a rock by a child."
• by = WHO did it  |  with = WHAT was used
⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
❌
The book was wrote by her. → The book was written by her.
بعد was/were لازم past participle الصحيح — ليس past simple
❌
The report is being write. → The report is being written.
is being + past participle — ليس base verb
❌
The problem can be solving. → The problem can be solved.
modal + be + past participle — ليس verb-ing
❌
It is said that he be honest. → It is said that he is honest.
بعد that تأتي جملة كاملة بفعل مصرّف — ليس base verb
❌
She got promote last year. → She got promoted last year.
get passive = get + past participle — ليس base verb
❌
The door was opened by a key. → The door was opened with a key.
الأدوات والآلات تستخدم with — ليس by
💡 Quick Tips
modal + be
Modal passive: modal + be + pp — never changes: "must be done", "can be solved" 🔉المبني للمجهول مع Modal: modal + be (ثابتة) + past participle
Reporting verbs
"It is said that he is rich" = "He is said to be rich" — both correct 🔉الصيغتان صحيحتان — اختر أيهما يناسب السياق
get = informal
get passive is informal (speech/casual writing) — use be passive for formal/academic contexts 🔉get passive عامية / be passive رسمية — حسب السياق
by vs with
by = WHO (agent)  ·  with = WHAT tool was used: "broken by him / with a hammer" 🔉by = مَن فعل | with = الأداة التي استُخدمت
📰 Breaking News
خبر عاجل
Reporting passive — is said / is believed
Did you see the news? It is reported that a major deal has been signed between the two countries. 🔉
رأيت الأخبار؟ يُفيد التقرير بأن صفقة كبرى وُقِّعت بين البلدين.
Hana
Yes. The agreement is believed to be worth billions. It is expected that trade will increase significantly. 🔉
نعم. يُعتقد أن الاتفاقية تساوي مليارات. ومن المتوقع أن تزداد التجارة بشكل ملحوظ.
Tariq
It is also said that new jobs will be created in both countries within a year. 🔉
ويُقال أيضاً أن وظائف جديدة ستُستحدث في البلدين خلال عام.
Hana
The details are known to be complicated but the leaders are said to be optimistic. 🔉
يُعلم أن التفاصيل معقدة لكن يُقال إن القادة متفائلون.
Tariq
💼 Work Meeting
اجتماع عمل
Modal passive + Perfect passive
The new system must be installed before the end of the month. 🔉
يجب تثبيت النظام الجديد قبل نهاية الشهر.
Manager
All the old data has already been transferred. The new servers were set up last week. 🔉
تم نقل كل البيانات القديمة بالفعل. تم تركيب الخوادم الجديدة الأسبوع الماضي.
IT Lead
Good. All staff should be notified by email before the system is switched on. 🔉
جيد. يجب إشعار كل الموظفين عبر البريد قبل تشغيل النظام.
Manager
The emails will be sent tomorrow. Any issues can be reported directly to me. 🔉
ستُرسل الرسائل غداً. يمكن الإبلاغ عن أي مشكلة مباشرةً إليّ.
IT Lead
🚧 Road Works
أعمال الطريق
Continuous passive — is being / was being
Why is this road always so blocked in the mornings? 🔉
لماذا هذا الطريق مزدحم دائماً في الصباح؟
Rana
The whole underground system is being expanded. New lines are being built under the city. 🔉
يتم توسعة نظام المترو بأكمله. يتم بناء خطوط جديدة تحت المدينة.
Sami
I heard the old bridge was being repaired when it suddenly collapsed last year. 🔉
سمعت أن الجسر القديم كان يُصلَح حين انهار فجأة العام الماضي.
Rana
Yes. A new bridge is now being constructed in its place. It should be finished by spring. 🔉
نعم. يتم الآن بناء جسر جديد مكانه. يجب أن ينتهي بحلول الربيع.
Sami
💬 Office Gossip
ثرثرة المكتب
Reporting passive + perfect passive
Have you heard? It is said that the director is going to resign. 🔉
سمعت؟ يُقال أن المدير سيستقيل.
Noor
Really? He is thought to have been offered a much better position at another company. 🔉
حقاً؟ يُعتقد أنه عُرض عليه منصب أفضل بكثير في شركة أخرى.
Lara
I heard that a decision has already been made. The announcement will be made on Friday. 🔉
سمعت أن القرار اتُّخذ فعلاً. سيُعلن عنه يوم الجمعة.
Noor
Well, until it is confirmed officially, nothing can be believed. 🔉
حسناً، حتى يُؤكَّد رسمياً، لا يمكن تصديق أي شيء.
Lara

أخبار جيدة في العمل

للتو حصلت على ترقية! كنت أعمل نحو هذا منذ سنتين.
Khalid
Congratulations! You really deserve it. Did you get a raise too? 🔉
مبروك! أنت تستحق ذلك فعلاً. هل حصلت على زيادة راتب أيضاً؟
Dina
Yes! And remember Ahmed? He got fired last month. Apparently he got caught lying on his reports. 🔉
نعم! وتذكرين أحمد؟ طُرد الشهر الماضي. على ما يبدو ضُبط وهو يكذب في تقاريره.

I'm not surprised. He always got confused between the rules and thought he wouldn't get caught. 🔉
لست مندهشة. كان دائماً يخلط الأمور ويظن أنه لن يُضبط.

📘 The Old Theatre
المسرح القديم

The Grand Theatre was built in 1903 and is considered one of the finest examples of early twentieth-century architecture in the region. 🔉

For many years the theatre was used as a cinema, but it was eventually closed in the 1980s when the building was found to be structurally unsafe. 🔉

It is reported that restoration plans were first proposed in 2010 but no funding could be found. The project is said to have been delayed several times due to disagreements over the budget. 🔉

Work finally began three years ago. The roof has been completely replaced, the original stage has been restored, and hundreds of seats have been reupholstered by hand. 🔉

At the moment, the entrance hall is still being painted and the lighting system is being upgraded. The project is expected to be completed by the end of the year. 🔉

It is hoped that the Grand Theatre will be reopened to the public next spring. The first performance is believed to have been booked already by a famous orchestra. 🔉
