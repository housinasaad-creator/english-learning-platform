📚 Unit 12

Future Plans

خطط المستقبل — going to / will / تعبيرات الزمن المستقبل

Learn to talk about your plans, make promises, and predict the future.

🔭 Future Plans — going to & will

نستخدم going to للخطط المسبقة والتوقعات المبنية على دليل، ونستخدم will للقرارات اللحظية والوعود والتوقعات

GOING TO للخطط المسبقة والأدلة · WILL للقرارات اللحظية والوعود والآراء

📅 Going To — الهيكل الكامل

نستخدم going to لخطط قررناها مسبقاً أو للتوقع بناءً على دليل واضح أمامنا

✅ Positive Subject + am / is / are + going to + base verb
❌ Negative Subject + am not / isn't / aren't + going to + base verb
❓ Question Am / Is / Are + Subject + going to + base verb ?
✅ Positive Examples
I am going to visit my parents this weekend.
أنا سأزور والديّ هذا الأسبوع.
She is going to study medicine at university.
هي ستدرس الطب في الجامعة.
They are going to move to a new city next year.
هم سينتقلون إلى مدينة جديدة العام القادم.
We are going to save money for a holiday.
سنوفّر المال لعطلة.
❌ Negative Examples
He isn't going to come to the party.
هو لن يأتي إلى الحفلة.
We aren't going to order pizza tonight.
لن نطلب بيتزا الليلة.
❓ Question Examples
Are you going to cook dinner tonight?
هل ستطبخ العشاء الليلة؟
Is she going to apply for the job?
هل ستتقدم للوظيفة؟
💡 الإجابات القصيرة: Yes, I am. / No, she isn't. / Yes, they are. / No, we aren't.
🔍 استخدام going to للتوقع بدليل
Look at those clouds! It is going to rain.
انظر إلى تلك الغيوم! ستمطر. (دليل: الغيوم)
He is running so fast. He is going to win the race!
هو يجري بسرعة كبيرة. سيفوز بالسباق! (دليل: الجري السريع)

⚡ Will — الهيكل الكامل

نستخدم will للقرارات اللحظية والوعود والعروض وكذلك للتوقعات والآراء عن المستقبل

✅ Positive Subject + will (= 'll) + base verb لا to بعد will
❌ Negative Subject + won't + base verb won't = will not
❓ Question Will + Subject + base verb ?
⚡ Spontaneous Decisions — قرار لحظي
The phone is ringing. I'll answer it.
الهاتف يرن. سأرد عليه. (قرار الآن)
I'm hungry. I'll make a sandwich.
أنا جائع. سأصنع شطيرة. (قرار الآن)
🤝 Offers & Promises — عروض ووعود
Don't worry. I'll help you with your homework.
لا تقلق. سأساعدك في واجبك. (عرض)
I promise I'll never tell anyone your secret.
أعدك، لن أخبر أحداً بسرّك. (وعد)
💭 Predictions & Opinions — توقعات وآراء
I think she'll pass the exam. She studied hard.
أعتقد أنها ستنجح في الامتحان.
It will probably be sunny tomorrow.
من المحتمل أن يكون الجو مشمساً غداً.
He won't be late. He is always on time.
لن يتأخر. هو دائماً في الوقت.
💡 الإجابات القصيرة: Will you come? — Yes, I will. / No, I won't.

⚖️ Going To vs Will — متى نستخدم كل واحد؟

الموقفالأداةمثال
خطة قررتها قبل الكلامgoing to"I'm going to visit my aunt tomorrow." (قرار سابق)
قرار لحظي عند الكلامwill"The phone's ringing — I'll get it!" (قرار الآن)
توقع بدليل مرئيgoing to"Look! It's going to snow!" (السماء ملبدة)
رأي أو توقع عامwill"I think it will rain tomorrow." (رأي شخصي)
عرض المساعدةwill"I'll carry that for you." (عرض لحظي)
وعدwill"I'll always be there for you." (وعد)
🔑 الفرق الأساسي: going to = قبل هذه اللحظة · will = في هذه اللحظة
Going to: "I'm going to cook pasta tonight." (bought ingredients already)
خطة مسبقة — اشترى المكونات بالفعل
Will: "I'm hungry. I'll make some pasta." (decided just now)
قرار لحظي — قررها الآن للتو

🗓 Future Time Expressions — تعبيرات الزمن المستقبل

هذه الكلمات تخبرنا متى سيحدث الشيء في المستقبل

Englishعربيمثال
tomorrowغداًI'll call you tomorrow.

💡 Tips — نصائح مهمة

1. After will, use the base form — no "to"!
بعد will نستخدم الفعل الأساسي مباشرة — لا to
2. Contractions: I'll / She'll / They'll / won't
نختصر will في الكلام: I will → I'll · will not → won't
3. "Gonna" = going to (spoken only — avoid in writing!)
gonna تُستخدم في الكلام فقط — لا في الكتابة الرسمية
4. In time clauses (when / after / before / as soon as) — use Present Simple, NOT will or going to
في جمل الوقت — نستخدم المضارع البسيط وليس will
✅ I'll call you when I arrive.   ❌ when I will arrive.
نستخدم Present Simple في جملة الوقت
✅ As soon as she finishes, she'll call us.
as soon as + مضارع — ليس will

⚠️ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة

I go to buy a new phone tomorrow.
I'm going to buy a new phone tomorrow.
going to يحتاج am/is/are قبله — لا يُستخدم فعل go مباشرة
She will to study tonight.
She will study tonight.
بعد will نضع الفعل الأساسي مباشرة بدون to
We going to travel next week.
We are going to travel next week.
لازم فعل be (am/is/are) قبل going to
I will going to cook dinner.
I'm going to cook dinner. / I will cook dinner.
will و going to لا يُستخدمان معاً في نفس الجملة
He don't will come to the party.
He won't come to the party.
النفي مع will هو won't وليس don't/doesn't will
Will you going to join us?
Are you going to join us?
السؤال مع going to يستخدم am/is/are — وليس will

Dialogue 01
🎉 Weekend Plans
Sam (right) · Jay (left)
Sam
What are you going to do this weekend?
ماذا ستفعل هذا الأسبوع؟
Jay
I'm going to visit my parents in Bristol. What about you?
سأزور والديّ في بريستول. وأنت؟
Sam
I haven't decided yet. I think I'll stay at home and watch a film.
لم أقرر بعد. أعتقد أنني سأبقى في البيت وأشاهد فيلماً.
Jay
That sounds relaxing! Are you going to cook something nice?
يبدو مريحاً! هل ستطبخ شيئاً لذيذاً؟
Sam
Maybe. I'll probably order a pizza, actually.
ربما. على الأرجح سأطلب بيتزا في الواقع.
Jay
Will you call me later this evening?
هل ستتصل بي لاحقاً هذا المساء؟
Sam
Sure, I'll call you at about seven. Have a great weekend!
بالتأكيد، سأتصل بك حوالي السابعة. أسبوع رائع!
Dialogue 02
✈️ At the Travel Agency
Agent (right) · Emma (left)
Agent
Good morning! How can I help you?
صباح الخير! كيف أستطيع مساعدتك؟
Emma
Hi! I'm going to travel to Italy next summer. Can you help me?
أهلاً! أنا سأسافر إلى إيطاليا الصيف القادم. هل يمكنك مساعدتي؟
Agent
Of course! Are you going to travel alone or with family?
بالطبع! هل ستسافرين وحدك أم مع العائلة؟

With my husband. We're going to spend ten days there.
مع زوجي. سنقضي عشرة أيام هناك.
Agent
Excellent. I'll find the best deals for you. Will you need a hotel?
ممتاز. سأجد أفضل العروض لك. هل ستحتاجين لفندق أيضاً؟
Emma
Yes please. We're going to stay in Rome for five days, then go to Florence.
نعم من فضلك. سنبقى في روما خمسة أيام ثم نذهب إلى فلورنسا.
Agent
Great choice! I'll send you all the details by email this afternoon.
خيار رائع! سأرسل لك جميع التفاصيل على الإيميل بعد الظهر.

📖 Reading — Maya's Big Year

اقرأ النص — going to و will بالخط العريض · خطط مايا للسنة القادمة

Maya is 22 years old and she has just finished university. She is very excited because she has many plans for the year ahead.

✈️ The Gap Year Trip

"I'm going to take a gap year before I start work," she says. "First, I'm going to travel to Southeast Asia with my friend Sara. We're going to visit Thailand, Vietnam, and Cambodia. We'll stay in each country for about two weeks. I think it's going to be an amazing experience!"

💼 The New Job

After her trip, Maya is going to start a new job. She has already found a position at a marketing company in London. "I'll move to London in September," she says. "My aunt lives there, so I'll stay with her at first. After a few months, I'm going to look for my own flat."

🎯 Personal Goals

Maya also has some personal goals. "I'm going to join a gym and get fit," she says. "I'll probably start running too. And I'm definitely going to save money every month. I want to buy a car by the end of the year."

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Support

Her family is very supportive. "My parents will help me if I need them," she says. "But I think I'll manage on my own. It's going to be a great year. I can't wait!"

📊 Going To vs Will in the Text

going to للخطط المقررة سلفاً · will للتوقعات والوعود والآراء اللحظية

❓ Comprehension Questions — أسئلة الفهم

اختر الإجابة الصحيحة

1. What is Maya going to do before she starts work?

"I'm going to take a gap year before I start work."

2. Which countries is Maya going to visit?

"We're going to visit Thailand, Vietnam, and Cambodia."

3. When is Maya going to move to London?

"I'll move to London in September."

4. Where will Maya stay when she first arrives in London?

"My aunt lives there, so I'll stay with her at first."

5. What is one of Maya's personal goals?

"I'm definitely going to save money every month."

6. What does Maya say about managing on her own?

"I think I'll manage on my own."
