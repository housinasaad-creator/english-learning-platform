Reported Speech

الكلام غير المباشر

Learn how to report what someone said without quoting them directly — covering tense backshift, pronoun changes, time expression shifts, reporting questions and commands, and key reporting verbs.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
📌 What is Reported Speech? — ما هو الكلام غير المباشر؟
ننقل ما قاله شخص ما بكلامنا الخاص — بدون علامات اقتباس
🔵 Direct Speech — مباشر
She said, "I am tired."
كلام مباشر بين علامات اقتباس
→
🟢 Reported Speech — غير مباشر
She said (that) she was tired.
كلام غير مباشر — يتغير الزمن والضمير
Key word: كلمة that اختيارية بعد said/told — يمكن حذفها:
She said that she was tired. = She said she was tired. ✅
⏪ Tense Backshift — تراجع الزمن
عند نقل الكلام، يتراجع زمن الفعل درجة واحدة إلى الماضي
# 🔵 Direct (زمن) 🟢 Reported (يصبح) 📋 Example 🔉
1 Present Simple Past Simple "I work here." → He said he worked there. 🔉المضارع → الماضي
2 Present Continuous Past Continuous "I am studying." → She said she was studying. 🔉المضارع المستمر → الماضي المستمر
3 Past Simple Past Perfect "I finished it." → He said he had finished it. 🔉الماضي البسيط → الماضي التام
4 Present Perfect Past Perfect "I have eaten." → She said she had eaten. 🔉المضارع التام → الماضي التام
5 will would "I will call." → He said he would call. 🔉will → would
6 can could "I can swim." → She said she could swim. 🔉can → could
7 must had to "I must leave." → He said he had to leave. 🔉must → had to
8 should / would / could no change "I should go." → She said she should go. 🔉هذه الأفعال لا تتغير

❓ Reported Questions — نقل الأسئلة
عند نقل الأسئلة — نستخدم ترتيب الجملة الخبرية (Subject + Verb) وليس ترتيب السؤال
🔵 Yes/No Questions — أسئلة بـ if / whether
Direct: "Are you tired?" → Reported: She asked if / whether I was tired.
Direct: "Do you like coffee?" → He asked if I liked coffee.
نستخدم if أو whether بدلاً من الفراغ — وترتيب الكلمات يصبح عادياً
🟣 Wh- Questions — أسئلة بكلمة استفهام
Direct: "Where do you live?" → He asked where I lived.
Direct: "What time does it start?" → She asked what time it started.
Direct: "Why are you late?" → He asked why I was late.
نحتفظ بكلمة الاستفهام — وترتيب الكلمات يصبح Wh + Subject + Verb (بدون انعكاس)
❓ Question example (Wh)
Direct
She asked, "What time does the train leave?"
"متى يغادر القطار؟" — سؤال مباشر
Reported
She asked what time the train left.
سألت عن وقت مغادرة القطار
📢 Reported Commands & Requests — نقل الأوامر والطلبات
الأوامر والطلبات تُنقل بصيغة: told/asked + someone + (not) to + infinitive
✅ Positive command
Direct: "Come here!" → He told me to go there.
أمر إيجابي → told + to + فعل
❌ Negative command
Direct: "Don't be late!" → He told her not to be late.
أمر منفي → told + not to + فعل
🙏 Request (please)
Direct: "Please help me." → She asked him to help her.
طلب بـ please → asked + to + فعل
🙏 Negative request
"Please don't make noise." → She asked them not to make noise.
طلب منفي → asked + not to + فعل
🗣 Reporting Verbs — أفعال النقل
بدل said يمكن استخدام أفعال أكثر دقة تعبّر عن طبيعة الكلام الأصلي
told
+ object + that — يلزمه مفعول به
She told me she was leaving.
explained
يشرح / يوضّح سبباً
He explained the machine was broken.
promised
وعد بشيء
She promised she would be on time.
warned
حذّر من خطر — + not to
He warned me not to touch it.
admitted
اعترف بشيء
She admitted she had made a mistake.
suggested
اقترح شيئاً — + -ing
He suggested going to the cinema.
refused
رفض — + to + infinitive
She refused to answer.
offered
عرض المساعدة — + to
He offered to carry the bags.
denied
نفى — + -ing
He denied stealing the money.
💡 Quick Tips
said vs told
She said she was tired. ✅  |  She told me she was tired. ✅ 🔉said لا يحتاج مفعولاً — told يلزمه مفعول به (me, him, her...)
No "?"
She asked where I lived. ✅   (NOT: where did I live?) 🔉الجملة المنقولة ليست سؤالاً — لا نضع علامة استفهام
No backshift
She said she loves Paris. ✅ (still true now — no need to shift) 🔉إذا كان الكلام لا يزال صحيحاً، يمكن عدم تراجع الزمن
"that" optional
He said (that) he was fine. — both are correct 🔉كلمة that اختيارية — يمكن حذفها في المحادثة العادية

📘 The Witness
الشاهد

Detective Harris opened his notebook and looked at the woman across the table. 🔉

She said that she had seen a man leaving the building at midnight. 🔉

The detective asked her what the man had looked like. She told him he was tall and was wearing a dark jacket. 🔉

Harris then asked whether she had seen his face clearly. She admitted that she had not been close enough. 🔉

She explained that the street light had been broken that night. 🔉

The detective warned her not to speak to anyone about what she had seen, and asked her to return the following day. 🔉
