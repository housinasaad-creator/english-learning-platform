Phrasal Verbs

مقدمة الأفعال المركبة

Learn the most common phrasal verbs in English — verb + particle combinations with new meanings. Understand the difference between separable and inseparable types, and master 25 essential phrasal verbs with real examples.

📖 Grammar 💬 Everyday English 📘 Reading ✅ Check It!
📌 What Is a Phrasal Verb? — ما هو الفعل المركب؟
فعل مركب = فعل عادي + جسيم (حرف جر أو ظرف) يُعطي معنىً جديداً مختلفاً
Verb + Particle = New Meaning
• The particle can be a preposition (up, off, out, in, on, away…) or an adverb
• Together they create a meaning you often cannot guess from the individual words
• Phrasal verbs are extremely common in spoken and informal English
"give" + "up" → give up = يستسلم (NOT "give" + "up in the air")  |  "look" + "after" → look after = يعتني بـ (NOT "look at something above")
🔢 Three Types of Phrasal Verbs — ثلاثة أنواع
النوع يحدد كيف تضع المفعول به في الجملة
Type 1
Intransitive
بدون مفعول به
No object needed after the phrasal verb
"She gave up."  |  "He broke down."  |  "They set off early." 🔉
Type 2
Separable
قابل للفصل — مع مفعول به
Object can go between verb + particle OR after the particle
"Turn off the lights." = "Turn the lights off." 🔉
Type 3
Inseparable
غير قابل للفصل
Object MUST come after the full phrasal verb — never between
"Look after the children." ✅   "Look the children after." ❌ 🔉
⚠ The Pronoun Rule for Separable Verbs — قاعدة الضمائر
إذا كان المفعول به ضميراً (it / them / him / her…) مع فعل مركب قابل للفصل — يجب الفصل دائماً
📍 Separable + Full Noun — يمكن وضعه في أي مكان
✅
Turn off the TV.  /  Turn the TV off. — both correct
✅
Pick up the phone.  /  Pick the phone up. — both correct
⚠ Separable + PRONOUN — يجب الفصل
✅ "Turn it off."  (pronoun MUST go in the middle)
❌ "Turn off it."  (WRONG — never put pronoun after the particle)
✅ "Pick them up."  |  "Give it up."  |  "Put it on."
❌ "Pick up them."  |  "Give up it."  |  "Put on it."

⚠ Common Mistakes — أخطاء شائعة
هذه الأخطاء شائعة حتى عند المتعلمين المتقدمين
❌
Give up it.  →  Give it up.
مع الضمائر + الأفعال القابلة للفصل — يجب وضع الضمير في الوسط
❌
Look the children after.  →  Look after the children.
look after غير قابل للفصل — المفعول يأتي دائماً بعد الفعل المركب كاملاً
❌
I look forward to see you.  →  I look forward to seeing you.
to في look forward to هو حرف جر — يليه الفعل بصيغة -ing وليس المصدر
❌
Turn off it.  →  Turn it off.
نفس القاعدة — الضمير لا يأتي بعد الجسيم أبداً
❌
I ran into him accidentally.  →  I ran into him. ✅ (accidentally is already implied)
run into تعني المصادفة بطبيعتها — لا حاجة لإضافة accidentally
💡 Quick Tips — نصائح سريعة
Meaning
You cannot guess the meaning from individual words — learn each phrasal verb as a single unit. 🔉المعنى لا يُستنتج من مجموع الكلمات — احفظ كل فعل مركب ككتلة واحدة
Pronouns
With pronouns + separable verbs: ALWAYS split — "Turn it off." / "Pick them up." 🔉الضمير مع الفعل القابل للفصل → يجب الفصل دائماً
look forward to
"look forward to" + -ing: "I look forward to meeting you." (NOT "to meet") 🔉to هنا حرف جر وليس جزء من المصدر — يليه الفعل بصيغة -ing
Multi-meaning
Some phrasal verbs have multiple meanings: "pick up" = collect / learn quickly / answer the phone 🔉بعض الأفعال المركبة لها أكثر من معنى — السياق هو الذي يُحدد المعنى

📘 A New Beginning
بداية جديدة

When Layla moved to a new city, she decided not to give up on her dream of becoming a chef. 🔉

At first, things were difficult. She had to figure out how the local transport system worked and set up a new bank account. 🔉

She came across a cooking school while walking in her new neighbourhood and decided to look into it. 🔉

On her first day, she ran into an old friend from university who was also studying there. They got along immediately and worked on projects together. 🔉

There were tough moments. Once, her oven broke down during a timed test and she almost gave up. But she carried on, picked up new skills fast, and handed in the best dish of the class. 🔉

By the end of the year, Layla had set up her own small catering business. She looked forward to every new day and never put off the hard work that success required. 🔉
